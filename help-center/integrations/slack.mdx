## Overview

Connect Smartbills to Slack to receive real-time notifications about expense reports, approvals, and team activity.

## Setting Up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Go to **Integrations** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Slack">
    Search for **Slack** and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize">
    Sign in to your Slack workspace and authorize Smartbills.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Channel">
    Select which Slack channel should receive notifications.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Events">
    Choose which events trigger Slack notifications:
    - Expense report submitted
    - Expense report approved / rejected
    - New expense uploaded
    - Reimbursement processed
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Notification Examples

- "**John Doe** submitted expense report *Q1 Travel* ($1,250.00) for your approval"
- "Expense report *Q1 Travel* was **approved** by Sarah Manager"
- "3 new expenses uploaded by the engineering team"
