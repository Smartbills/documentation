## Overview

Sync approved expenses from Smartbills to Xero for automated bookkeeping and reconciliation.

## Connecting Xero

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Go to **Integrations** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Xero">
    Search for **Xero** and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize">
    Sign in to Xero and grant Smartbills access to your organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map Accounts">
    Map Smartbills categories to Xero chart of accounts and configure tax tracking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable">
    Save your configuration. Approved expenses will sync based on your preferences.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Syncs

| Smartbills | Xero |
|-----------|------|
| Approved expenses | Bank transactions / Spend money |
| Categories | Account codes |
| Vendors | Contacts |
| Tax amounts | Tax rates |
| Attachments | Transaction files |

## Disconnecting

To disconnect Xero, go to **Integrations** → **Xero** → **Disconnect**. Existing synced data in Xero is not affected.
