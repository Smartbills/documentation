## Overview

Sync your Smartbills expenses, categories, and vendors with QuickBooks Online for seamless accounting.

## Connecting QuickBooks

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Go to **Integrations** (or **Apps**) in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find QuickBooks">
    Search for **QuickBooks** in the marketplace and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize">
    Sign in to your QuickBooks account and authorize Smartbills to access your data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Mapping">
    Map your Smartbills categories to QuickBooks expense accounts:
    - Smartbills categories → QuickBooks accounts
    - Vendors → QuickBooks vendors
    - Tax codes → QuickBooks tax rates
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Sync">
    Choose your sync preferences:
    - **Automatic** — Approved expenses sync automatically
    - **Manual** — Export expenses to QuickBooks on demand
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Syncs

| Smartbills | QuickBooks |
|-----------|------------|
| Approved expenses | Expense transactions |
| Expense categories | Expense accounts |
| Vendors | Vendor records |
| Tax amounts | Tax line items |
| Receipt attachments | Transaction attachments |

## Troubleshooting

- **Auth expired**: Reconnect from the integrations page
- **Category not mapping**: Ensure the QuickBooks account exists and is active
- **Duplicate entries**: Check the sync date range to avoid re-syncing
