## Overview

Use Zapier to connect Smartbills with thousands of other apps — create automated workflows without writing code.

## Setting Up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Go to **Integrations** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Zapier">
    Search for **Zapier** and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize">
    Connect your Zapier account with your Smartbills API credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Zaps">
    In Zapier, create automations using Smartbills as a trigger or action.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Popular Zaps

| Trigger | Action | Use Case |
|---------|--------|----------|
| New expense in Smartbills | Create row in Google Sheets | Expense tracking spreadsheet |
| Expense report approved | Send email via Gmail | Notify finance team |
| New expense uploaded | Post to Slack channel | Real-time team updates |
| Report reimbursed | Update Airtable record | Reimbursement tracking |

## Available Triggers

## Available Actions
