## Overview

Connect your bank accounts via Plaid to automatically import transactions and match them with uploaded receipts.

## Connecting a Bank Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Vendor Connections">
    Go to **Vendors** and select a vendor, or navigate to payment method settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect Bank">
    Click **Connect Bank Account** to start the Plaid linking flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Your Bank">
    Search for your bank and select it from the list. Plaid supports thousands of financial institutions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign In">
    Enter your bank credentials securely through Plaid's interface. Smartbills never sees your bank login.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Accounts">
    Choose which accounts to link (checking, credit card, etc.).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Complete the connection. Transactions begin importing within minutes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Transaction Matching

Once connected, Smartbills automatically:

- Imports new transactions daily
- Suggests matches between bank transactions and uploaded receipts
- Highlights unmatched transactions for review

## Security

- Bank credentials are handled exclusively by Plaid — Smartbills never stores them
- Connections use bank-level encryption
- You can disconnect at any time from the integrations page
