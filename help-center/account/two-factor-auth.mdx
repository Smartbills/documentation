## Overview

Two-factor authentication adds an extra layer of security by requiring a second verification step when signing in.

## Mobile Biometric Lock

The Smartbills mobile app supports biometric authentication:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Security Settings">
    Go to **Menu** → **Security**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Biometrics">
    Toggle on **Face ID** (iOS) or **Fingerprint** (Android) to require biometric verification when opening the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Authenticate with your biometric to confirm the setting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Password Security

For additional security:

- Use a strong, unique password for your Smartbills account
- Avoid reusing passwords from other services
- Change your password regularly from [account settings](/help-center/account/reset-password)

<Note>
Full TOTP-based two-factor authentication is coming soon. Contact support for enterprise SSO options.
</Note>
