---
title: "Delete Your Account"
description: "Permanently close your Smartbills account and remove all associated data"
---

## Overview

Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. All your personal data, expenses, and reports will be removed.

<Warning>
Before deleting your account, make sure to export any data you need. This action is irreversible.
</Warning>

## Before You Delete

- **Export your data** — Download expense reports and receipts you want to keep
- **Leave businesses** — You must leave all businesses before deleting your account
- **Cancel subscriptions** — Any active subscriptions will be cancelled

## Mobile App

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Account Settings">
    Go to **Menu** → **Account** → **Personal Information**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scroll to Delete">
    Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete Account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provide Feedback">
    Optionally provide a reason for leaving to help us improve.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Deletion">
    Type your email address to confirm and tap **Delete My Account**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Gets Deleted

| Data | Deleted |
|------|---------|
| Personal profile and preferences | Yes |
| Personal expenses and receipts | Yes |
| Business expenses you created | No — transferred to business |
| Business memberships | Removed |
| Payment methods | Yes |

<Note>
After deletion, you can create a new account with the same email address, but none of your previous data will be available.
</Note>
