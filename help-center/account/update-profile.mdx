---

## Overview

Keep your profile information up to date to ensure proper identification across your businesses and expense reports.

## Web Dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click your avatar in the bottom-left corner of the sidebar, then select **Profile** from the menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit Your Information">
    Update any of the following fields:
    - **First Name** and **Last Name**
    - **Email Address**
    - **Phone Number**
    - **Profile Picture** — Click the avatar to upload a new photo
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Changes">
    Click **Save** to apply your changes. Your updated name and avatar will appear across all businesses you belong to.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Mobile App

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Menu">
    Tap the **Menu** tab (bottom right) in the navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Your Name">
    Tap your name or avatar at the top of the menu screen to open your profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit Fields">
    Update your name, email, phone number, or profile picture.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Tap **Save** in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Your profile information is shared across all businesses. Updating your name here will reflect everywhere.
</Tip>
