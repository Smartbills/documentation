## Overview

Business administrators can manage team members, send invitations, and control access to the platform.

## Inviting Team Members

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team Settings">
    Go to **Settings** → **Team** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite">
    Click the **Invite Member** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Details">
    Provide the team member's:
    - **Email address**
    - **Role** (Administrator, Finance Admin, Manager, Member, Employee)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send Invitation">
    Click **Send Invite**. The recipient receives an email with a link to join your business.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing Existing Members

### Change a Member's Role

1. Open **Settings** → **Team**
2. Find the member in the list
3. Click on their name or the edit icon
4. Select a new role from the dropdown
5. Save changes

### Deactivate a Member

1. Click the member's options menu (⋯)
2. Select **Deactivate**
3. The member loses access but their expense data is preserved

### Remove a Member

1. Click the member's options menu (⋯)
2. Select **Remove**
3. Confirm the removal

<Warning>
Removing a member is different from deactivating. Deactivation preserves the member's data and allows reactivation later.
</Warning>

## Invitation Statuses

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Pending** | Invitation sent, awaiting response |
| **Accepted** | Member has joined the business |
| **Expired** | Invitation was not accepted in time |

You can resend expired or pending invitations from the team management page.
