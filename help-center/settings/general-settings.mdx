## Overview

General settings control your business's basic information, preferences, and configuration. This guide covers all general settings available to business administrators.

<Note>
  **Admin access required**: Most business settings require administrator or owner permissions.
</Note>

## Accessing Settings

### On Web

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Settings">
    1. Click your business name (top left)
    2. Select **Settings** from the dropdown
    3. Or click the gear icon ⚙️ in the sidebar
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Select General">
    1. In the settings sidebar, click **General**
    2. This is usually the first section
  </Step>
</Steps>

### On Mobile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    1. Tap **More** or **Profile** tab (bottom navigation)
    2. Tap **Business Settings**
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="View General Settings">
    1. Tap **General** at the top of the list
    2. View and edit settings
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Business Profile

### Business Name

**What it is:** Your company's official name as it appears throughout Smartbills

**How to change:**
1. Go to Settings → General
2. Find **Business Name** field
3. Click to edit
4. Enter new name
5. Click **Save Changes**

<Warning>
  **Important**: Changing your business name updates it everywhere in Smartbills, including reports and exports.
</Warning>

### Business Logo

**What it is:** Your company logo displayed on reports and invoices

**Supported formats:**
- PNG (recommended)
- JPG/JPEG
- SVG
- Max size: 5MB
- Recommended dimensions: 400x400px or larger

**How to upload:**
1. Go to Settings → General
2. Click **Upload Logo** or click existing logo
3. Choose file from your computer
4. Crop/adjust if needed
5. Click **Save**

<img
  className="block"
  src="/images/help-center/settings/upload-logo.png"
  alt="Upload business logo"
/>

<Tip>
  **Pro tip**: Use a square logo with transparent background (PNG) for best results across all platforms.
</Tip>

### Business Address

**What it's used for:**
- Appears on expense reports
- Used for tax calculations
- Required for some integrations
- Displayed on exported documents

**How to update:**
1. Go to Settings → General → **Address**
2. Enter or update:
   - Street address
   - City
   - State/Province
   - ZIP/Postal code
   - Country
3. Click **Save**

### Contact Information

**Email Address:**
- Primary business contact email
- Used for notifications
- Shown on reports

**Phone Number:**
- Business contact number
- Optional but recommended
- Format: +1 (555) 123-4567

**Website:**
- Company website URL
- Optional
- Displayed on reports

## Regional Settings

### Default Currency

**What it is:** The primary currency for your business

**Supported currencies:** 150+ currencies including:
- USD (US Dollar)
- CAD (Canadian Dollar)
- EUR (Euro)
- GBP (British Pound)
- JPY (Japanese Yen)
- And many more...

**How to change:**
1. Go to Settings → General → **Currency**
2. Select from dropdown
3. Click **Save**

<Warning>
  **Changing currency**: This only affects NEW expenses. Existing expenses keep their original currency. Consider carefully before changing.
</Warning>

**Multi-currency support:**
- Enable in Settings → Currency → **Multi-Currency**
- Allows expenses in different currencies
- Automatic exchange rate conversion
- See [Currency Settings](/help-center/settings/currency) for details

### Timezone

**What it's used for:**
- Timestamps on expenses and reports
- Scheduling automated reports
- Deadline calculations
- Email notification timing

**How to set:**
1. Go to Settings → General → **Timezone**
2. Search for your city or select timezone
3. Click **Save**

**Common timezones:**
- America/New_York (EST/EDT)
- America/Los_Angeles (PST/PDT)
- America/Chicago (CST/CDT)
- America/Toronto (EST/EDT)
- Europe/London (GMT/BST)
- Europe/Paris (CET/CEST)

### Date Format

**Options:**
- MM/DD/YYYY (US format)
- DD/MM/YYYY (International format)
- YYYY-MM-DD (ISO format)

**How to change:**
1. Go to Settings → General → **Date Format**
2. Select preferred format
3. Click **Save**

**Affects:**
- Date displays throughout the app
- Exported reports
- Email notifications

### Number Format

**Options:**
- 1,234.56 (US/UK format)
- 1.234,56 (European format)
- 1 234,56 (French format)

**How to change:**
1. Go to Settings → General → **Number Format**
2. Select format
3. Click **Save**

## Expense Policies

### Receipt Requirements

**Configure when receipts are required:**

**Options:**
- All expenses (strictest)
- Expenses over $25
- Expenses over $50
- Expenses over $75
- Never required (most lenient)

**How to set:**
1. Go to Settings → General → **Receipt Policy**
2. Select threshold
3. Click **Save**

<Tip>
  **IRS guideline**: The IRS requires receipts for expenses over $75. Many companies set this as their threshold.
</Tip>

### Expense Submission Deadline

**What it is:** How long after an expense date it can be submitted

**Options:**
- No deadline
- 30 days
- 60 days
- 90 days
- End of month
- End of quarter
- Custom

**How to set:**
1. Go to Settings → General → **Submission Deadline**
2. Select or enter deadline
3. Click **Save**

**Enforcement:**
- Soft warning (allows submission with warning)
- Hard block (prevents submission)

### Approval Requirements

**Configure approval workflow:**

**Options:**
- Single approver (manager only)
- Multi-level approval
- Amount-based routing
- Department-based routing

**See:** [Approval Workflows](/help-center/settings/approval-workflow) for detailed configuration

## Notifications

### Email Notifications

**Configure which emails your business receives:**

**Available notifications:**
- ✉️ New expense submitted
- ✉️ Report needs approval
- ✉️ Report approved
- ✉️ Report rejected
- ✉️ Policy violation detected
- ✉️ Weekly summary
- ✉️ Monthly summary

**How to configure:**
1. Go to Settings → General → **Notifications**
2. Toggle each notification type
3. Set recipients (admins, managers, all users)
4. Click **Save**

### Notification Recipients

**Who receives notifications:**
- **Admins only** - Business administrators
- **Managers** - All users with approval permissions
- **All users** - Everyone in the business

**Custom recipients:**
- Add specific email addresses
- Useful for accounting team, finance department
- Multiple addresses supported

## Danger Zone

### Transfer Ownership

**Transfer business ownership to another user:**

<Warning>
  **Permanent action**: This cannot be undone. The new owner will have full control.
</Warning>

**How to transfer:**
1. Go to Settings → General → **Danger Zone**
2. Click **Transfer Ownership**
3. Select new owner from user list
4. Enter your password to confirm
5. Click **Transfer**

**Requirements:**
- New owner must be an existing admin
- You must be current owner
- Requires password confirmation

### Delete Business

**Permanently delete your business and all data:**

<Warning>
  **PERMANENT**: This action cannot be undone. All data will be permanently deleted including:
  - All expenses
  - All reports
  - All users
  - All attachments
  - All settings
</Warning>

**How to delete:**
1. Go to Settings → General → **Danger Zone**
2. Click **Delete Business**
3. Read the warning carefully
4. Type business name to confirm
5. Enter your password
6. Click **Permanently Delete**

**Before deleting:**
- Export all data you need
- Download important reports
- Notify all users
- Cancel any subscriptions

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can't save changes" icon="circle-exclamation">
    **Possible reasons:**
    
    1. **Insufficient permissions**
       - Only admins can change most settings
       - Contact your business owner
    
    2. **Invalid data**
       - Check for error messages
       - Ensure required fields are filled
       - Verify format (email, phone, etc.)
    
    3. **Network issues**
       - Check internet connection
       - Try refreshing the page
       - Clear browser cache
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Settings not applying" icon="rotate">
    **Try these:**
    
    1. **Refresh the page** - Settings may need a refresh
    2. **Log out and back in** - Clear cached settings
    3. **Check other users** - Verify settings apply to everyone
    4. **Wait a moment** - Some changes take a few minutes
    
    If still not working, contact support.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Lost access to settings" icon="lock">
    **If you can't access settings:**
    
    1. **Check your role** - You need admin permissions
    2. **Contact owner** - Ask to be made an admin
    3. **Business deleted** - Verify business still exists
    
    Business owners can manage user roles in Settings → Users.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Best Practices

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete Your Profile">
    Fill in all business information completely:
    - Accurate address for tax purposes
    - Logo for professional reports
    - Contact info for support
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Set Clear Policies">
    Establish clear expense policies:
    - Receipt requirements
    - Submission deadlines
    - Approval workflows
    - Spending limits
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Configure Notifications">
    Set up appropriate notifications:
    - Keep admins informed
    - Don't overwhelm users
    - Use weekly summaries
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Regular Reviews">
    Review settings periodically:
    - Quarterly policy review
    - Annual fiscal year check
    - Update contact information
    - Review integrations
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Document Changes">
    Keep a record of setting changes:
    - Who made the change
    - When it was made
    - Why it was changed
    - Communicate to users
  </Step>
</Steps>
