## Overview

Departments help you organize employees and track expenses by organizational unit — such as Engineering, Marketing, or Sales.

## Creating a Department

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Go to **Settings** → **Departments**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create">
    Click **Create Department**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Details">
    Provide:
    - **Name** — e.g., "Engineering", "Marketing"
    - **Description** — Optional context
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create** to add the department.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Assigning Employees to Departments

1. Go to **Employees** in the sidebar
2. Click on an employee's name
3. Select a **Department** from the dropdown
4. Save changes

You can also bulk-assign departments from the employees list using the bulk action toolbar.

## Department Reports

Departments enable you to:

- **Filter expenses** by department in the expense list
- **Generate reports** showing spending per department
- **Set budgets** per department (with approval workflows)
- **Track trends** in the analytics dashboard

<Tip>
Set up approval workflows per department so each department head automatically reviews their team's expense reports.
</Tip>
