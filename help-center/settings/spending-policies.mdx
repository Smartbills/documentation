## Overview

Spending policies help enforce expense guidelines and flag out-of-policy expenses for review.

## Policy Types

| Policy | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Per-expense limit** | Maximum amount for a single expense |
| **Category limits** | Maximum amount per category per period |
| **Receipt required** | Require receipt attachments above a threshold |
| **Pre-approval** | Require approval before the expense is incurred |

## Configuring Policies

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Go to **Settings** → **General** or the policies section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define Rules">
    Configure your spending policies:
    - Set maximum expense amounts
    - Require receipts for expenses above a threshold (e.g., $25)
    - Define category-specific limits
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Enforcement">
    Choose how violations are handled:
    - **Warning** — The employee is notified but can still submit
    - **Block** — The expense is flagged and requires manager override
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save and apply the policies. They take effect on new submissions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Policy Violation Handling

When an expense violates a policy:

1. The employee sees a warning when submitting
2. The expense is flagged in the report
3. Approvers see the violation highlighted during review
4. The approver can override or reject the flagged expense

<Tip>
Communicate your spending policies to your team before enabling enforcement to avoid confusion.
</Tip>
