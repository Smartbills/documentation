## Overview

The Smartbills mobile app turns your phone's camera into a powerful receipt scanner with OCR technology.

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the App">
    Launch Smartbills on your iOS or Android device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the Upload Button">
    Tap the camera icon in the center of the bottom navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Method">
    Select one of:
    - **Camera** — Take a photo of a physical receipt
    - **Gallery** — Select an existing photo
    - **Files** — Upload a PDF or document
  </Step>
  <Step title="Capture">
    If using camera, align the receipt and tap capture. The app detects edges automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Processing">
    The app extracts vendor name, date, amounts, taxes, and line items using OCR.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review & Save">
    Verify the extracted data, assign a category, and save the expense.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Best Practices

- Place receipts on a dark, flat surface for best contrast
- Ensure even lighting without shadows
- Capture the full receipt including header and totals
- Keep the phone steady during capture

## Supported Formats

| Format | Support |
|--------|---------|
| Camera capture (JPEG) | Full |
| Gallery photos (JPEG, PNG) | Full |
| PDF documents | Full |
| HEIC (iOS) | Full |

<Tip>
For faded thermal receipts, try increasing brightness or using the flash for better OCR results.
</Tip>
