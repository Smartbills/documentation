## Overview

The Smartbills mobile app works offline so you can scan and save receipts anywhere, even without connectivity.

## How It Works

1. **Scan receipts** — Capture receipts normally using the camera
2. **Data queued** — Photos and expense data are stored locally on your device
3. **Auto-sync** — When you regain connectivity, everything syncs automatically
4. **Processing** — OCR processing happens once the data reaches our servers

## What Works Offline

| Feature | Offline Support |
|---------|----------------|
| Scan/capture receipts | Yes |
| Save expense drafts | Yes |
| View cached expenses | Yes |
| OCR processing | No — queued until online |
| Submit reports | No — queued until online |
| Push notifications | No |

## Syncing

When your device reconnects to the internet:

- Queued receipts upload automatically in the background
- OCR processing begins on the server
- You receive notifications when processing completes
- Any pending report submissions are sent

<Tip>
Check the upload queue in the app to see how many items are waiting to sync. A badge indicator shows pending uploads.
</Tip>

<Note>
Offline mode stores data locally on your device. Make sure you have sufficient storage space for receipt photos.
</Note>
