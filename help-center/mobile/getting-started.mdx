## Overview

The Smartbills mobile app brings powerful expense management to your fingertips. Scan receipts on the go, create expenses instantly, and manage your reports from anywhere.

<Note>
  **Platform Support:**
  - **iOS**: Requires iOS 14.0 or later
  - **Android**: Requires Android 8.0 or later
</Note>

## Download & Install

### iOS (iPhone & iPad)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the App Store">
    Tap the **App Store** icon on your home screen
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Search for Smartbills">
    Tap the search icon and type "Smartbills"
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Download">
    Tap **GET** next to the Smartbills app
    Authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID, or your Apple ID password
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Install">
    Wait for the app to download and install
    The app icon will appear on your home screen
  </Step>
</Steps>

<img
  className="block"
  src="/images/help-center/mobile/ios-download.png"
  alt="Download from App Store"
/>

### Android

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Google Play Store">
    Tap the **Play Store** icon
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Search for Smartbills">
    Use the search bar to find "Smartbills"
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Install">
    Tap **Install**
    The app will begin downloading
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Open">

## First-Time Setup

### Sign In or Create Account

When you first open the app:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Existing Account">
    **If you already have a Smartbills account:**
    
    1. Tap **Sign In**
    2. Enter your email and password
    3. Tap **Log In**
    4. Or use **Sign in with Google** or **Sign in with Apple** (iOS)
    
    <Check>
      You'll automatically sync all your existing expenses and reports!
    </Check>
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="New Account">
    **If you're new to Smartbills:**
    
    1. Tap **Create Account**
    2. Enter your information:
       - Email address
       - Password
       - First and last name
    3. Tap **Sign Up**
    4. Check your email for verification
    5. Tap the verification link
    6. Return to the app
    
    See the full guide: [Create an Account](/help-center/account/create-account)
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Grant Permissions

The app will request several permissions for full functionality:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Camera Permission (Required)" icon="camera">
    **Why we need it:** To scan receipts
    
    **iOS:**
    - Tap **OK** when prompted
    - Or go to Settings → Smartbills → Camera → Enable
    
    **Android:**
    - Tap **Allow** when prompted
    - Or go to Settings → Apps → Smartbills → Permissions → Camera → Allow
    
    <Warning>
      Without camera access, you won't be able to scan receipts. You can still upload from your photo library or enter expenses manually.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Photo Library Permission (Recommended)" icon="images">
    **Why we need it:** To upload existing receipt photos and save scanned images
    
    **iOS:**
    - Select **All Photos** or **Selected Photos**
    - You can change this later in Settings
    
    **Android:**
    - Tap **Allow** for storage access
    - This allows saving and accessing photos
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Notifications (Recommended)" icon="bell">
    **Why we need it:** To notify you about:
    - Report approvals
    - Comments on your expenses
    - Reimbursement updates
    - Policy violations
    
    **iOS:**
    - Tap **Allow** when prompted
    - Choose notification style in Settings
    
    **Android:**
    - Tap **Allow** when prompted
    - Configure notification channels in Settings
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Location Services (Optional)" icon="location-dot">
    **Why we need it:** To automatically tag expenses with GPS coordinates
    
    **iOS:**
    - Choose **While Using the App** (recommended)
    - Or **Never** if you prefer not to share location
    
    **Android:**
    - Select **While using the app** (recommended)
    - Or **Deny** if you don't want location tagging
    
    <Tip>
      Location tagging helps track expenses by location and can be useful for mileage tracking.
    </Tip>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Key Features

### Dashboard

Your command center shows:

- **Quick Stats**: Total expenses, pending reports, reimbursements
- **Recent Activity**: Latest expenses and updates
- **Pending Items**: Action items requiring attention
- **Quick Actions**: Fast access to common tasks

### Receipt Scanning

The app's killer feature:

1. **Smart Auto-Capture**: Camera detects receipts automatically
2. **Edge Detection**: Automatically crops to receipt boundaries
3. **OCR Extraction**: Reads merchant, amount, date instantly
4. **Multi-Receipt**: Scan multiple receipts in one session

<Tip>
  For best results:
  - Use good lighting
  - Flatten the receipt
  - Fill the frame
  - Avoid shadows and glare
</Tip>

### Offline Mode

Work without internet:

- ✅ Scan receipts offline

### Biometric Authentication

Secure and fast login:

**iOS (Face ID / Touch ID):**
1. Go to Settings → Security
2. Toggle **Enable Face ID** or **Enable Touch ID**
3. Authenticate to confirm
4. Next login will use biometrics

**Android (Fingerprint):**
1. Go to Settings → Security
2. Enable **Fingerprint Login**
3. Authenticate with your fingerprint
4. Biometric login is now active

<Check>
  **Recommended**: Enable biometric login for quick, secure access.
</Check>

## Tips for New Users

### Best Practices

<Steps>
  <Step title="Scan Receipts Immediately">
    Don't wait! Thermal receipts fade quickly. Scan them right after purchase.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Review OCR Data">
    Always verify automatically extracted information before saving.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Categorize Properly">
    Choose the correct category for accurate reporting and tax compliance.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Add Notes">
    Include context about business purpose, attendees, or project details.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Submit Regularly">
    Don't let expenses pile up. Create and submit reports weekly or monthly.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Enable Offline Mode">
    Download data for offline access when traveling.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Keyboard Shortcuts & Gestures

**iOS Gestures:**
- Swipe right on expense → Quick add to report
- Swipe left on expense → Delete or edit
- Pull down to refresh lists
- Long press on + button → Show all create options

**Android Gestures:**
- Swipe left/right on expenses → Actions
- Pull down to refresh
- Long press FAB → Quick actions menu

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="App won't open or keeps crashing" icon="circle-exclamation">
    **Try these solutions:**
    
    1. Force close and reopen the app
    2. Restart your device
    3. Check for app updates in the App Store/Play Store
    4. Ensure you have the latest OS version
    5. Clear app cache (Android)
    6. Reinstall the app (your data is safe in the cloud)
    
    Still not working? Contact [support@smartbills.io](mailto:support@smartbills.io)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Camera not working" icon="camera">
    **Check these:**
    
    1. Go to device Settings → Smartbills → Camera → Allow
    2. Ensure camera works in other apps
    3. Clean your camera lens
    4. Restart the app
    5. Restart your device
    
    See: [Mobile Troubleshooting](/help-center/mobile/troubleshooting)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Not receiving notifications" icon="bell">
    **Verify notification settings:**
    
    **iOS:**
    1. Settings → Notifications → Smartbills
    2. Ensure **Allow Notifications** is on
    3. Choose your preferred style
    
    **Android:**
    1. Settings → Apps → Smartbills → Notifications
    2. Ensure notifications are enabled
    3. Check individual notification channels
    
    Also check Do Not Disturb mode settings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Data not syncing" icon="arrows-rotate">
    **Sync troubleshooting:**
    
    1. Check internet connection
    2. Pull down to manually refresh
    3. Log out and log back in
    4. Check Settings → Sync Settings
    5. Ensure background app refresh is enabled (iOS)
    
    See: [Sync Issues](/help-center/troubleshooting/sync-issues)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

- Or enable automatic updates

**Android:**
- Play Store → My apps & games → Update Smartbills
- Or enable auto-update for all apps

  **New features**: Check the changelog in Settings → About to see what's new.
