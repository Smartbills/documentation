## Overview

When an employee submits an expense report, designated approvers receive a notification and can review, approve, reject, or request changes.

## Web Dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Approvals">
    Navigate to **Expense Report Approvals** in the sidebar. You'll see all reports awaiting your review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the Report">
    Click on a report to see:
    - List of expenses with amounts and categories
    - Attached receipts and documents
    - Employee notes and comments
    - Total amount and tax breakdown
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Individual Expenses">
    Click on each expense to verify the receipt image, amount, and category. Flag any issues.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take Action">
    Choose one of the following actions:
    - **Approve** — Accept the report as-is
    - **Reject** — Return the report with a reason
    - **Request Changes** — Ask the employee to modify specific items
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Mobile App

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Notifications">
    You'll receive a push notification when a report is submitted for your approval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Report">
    Tap the notification or navigate to the approvals section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and Decide">
    Review expenses and attachments, then tap **Approve**, **Reject**, or **Request Changes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Approval Statuses

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Pending** | Waiting for approver action |
| **Approved** | Report accepted, ready for reimbursement |
| **Rejected** | Report returned to employee |
| **Changes Requested** | Employee needs to modify and resubmit |
| **Reimbursed** | Payment has been processed |

<Tip>
Add a comment when approving or rejecting to give the employee context about your decision.
</Tip>
