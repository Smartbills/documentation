---
title: "Add Expenses to a Report"
description: "Add individual or multiple expenses to an expense report"
---

## Overview

After creating an expense report, add expenses to it before submitting for approval.

## Web Dashboard

### Add from Expense List

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Expenses">
    Go to **Wallet** or **Expenses** and select the expenses you want to add using the checkboxes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bulk Add to Report">
    Click **Add to Report** in the bulk action toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Report">
    Select an existing draft report or create a new one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Add from Report Detail

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Report">
    Go to **My Expense Reports** and click on the draft report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Expenses">
    Click the **Add Expenses** button within the report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Expenses">
    Browse available expenses (those not assigned to another report) and select the ones to include.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Add** to include the selected expenses in the report.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Mobile App

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Report">
    Navigate to **Reports** tab and tap on your draft report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Add">
    Tap the **+** button or **Add Expenses**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Expenses">
    Browse and select available expenses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Tap **Add** to include them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Removing Expenses

To remove an expense from a report, open the report, find the expense, and click/tap the **Remove** button. The expense returns to your unassigned expense list.

<Note>
You can only add or remove expenses from **draft** reports. Once submitted, the report is locked until recalled.
</Note>
