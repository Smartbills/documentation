---
title: "Recall an Expense Report"
description: "Pull back a submitted expense report to make changes before it's approved"
---

## Overview

If you submitted a report and need to make changes, you can recall it — as long as it hasn't been approved yet.

## Web Dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Report">
    Go to **My Expense Reports** and click on the submitted report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Recall">
    Click the **Recall** button. This is only available for reports with a **Submitted** or **Pending** status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Confirm that you want to recall the report. It returns to **Draft** status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make Changes">
    Edit expenses, add or remove items, update categories, then resubmit when ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Mobile App

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Report">
    Go to the **Reports** tab and tap on the submitted report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Recall">
    Tap **Recall** from the action menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit and Resubmit">
    Make your changes, then submit again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
You cannot recall a report that has already been **Approved** or **Reimbursed**. Contact your manager to have it returned if changes are needed.
</Warning>
