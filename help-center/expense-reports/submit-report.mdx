## Overview

Once you've created an expense report and added all your expenses, the next step is to submit it for approval. This guide walks you through the submission process and what happens after you submit.

<Note>
  **Before submitting**: Review your report carefully. Once submitted, you cannot edit expenses without recalling the report.
</Note>

## Pre-Submission Checklist

Before submitting your report, verify:

<Steps>
  <Step title="All Expenses Included">
    ✅ All eligible expenses for the period are added
    
    ✅ No expenses are missing
    
    ✅ Duplicates have been removed
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Receipts Attached">
    ✅ Every expense has a receipt image (per company policy)
    
    ✅ Receipts are clear and readable
    
    ✅ Multi-page receipts are complete
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Information Complete">
    ✅ All required fields are filled
    
    ✅ Categories are correct
    
    ✅ Amounts are accurate
    
    ✅ Dates are correct
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Business Purpose Documented">
    ✅ Notes explain business purpose
    
    ✅ Attendees listed for meals
    
    ✅ Project/client information added
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Policy Compliance">
    ✅ No policy violations (or explained)
    
    ✅ Amounts within limits
    
    ✅ Approved categories used
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Report Details">
    ✅ Report name is descriptive
    
    ✅ Description added
    
    ✅ Correct business/department selected
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **Policy violations**: If your report contains any policy violations, add explanatory notes before submitting.
</Warning>

## Submit on Web

### Standard Submission

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Your Report">
    1. Navigate to **Reports** in the sidebar
    2. Click on the draft report you want to submit
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Review Report Summary">
    Check the report summary at the top:
    - Total amount
    - Number of expenses
    - Date range
    - Policy violations (if any)
    
    <img
      className="block"
      src="/images/help-center/expense-reports/web-report-summary.png"
      alt="Report summary"
    />
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Review Each Expense">
    Scroll through the expense list and verify:
    - All expenses are correct
    - Receipts are attached
    - Categories are appropriate
    - Amounts are accurate
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Add Submission Notes (Optional)">
    1. Find the **Notes for Approver** section
    2. Add any context or explanations
    3. Explain policy violations if any
    
    Example notes:
    - "Client dinner with ABC Corp team"
    - "Hotel rate exceeded policy due to conference location"
    - "Urgent travel for emergency client meeting"
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Click Submit">
    1. Click the **Submit for Approval** button (top right)
    2. A confirmation dialog appears
    3. Review the submission details
    4. Click **Confirm Submit**
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Confirmation">
    ✅ You'll see a success message
    
    ✅ Report status changes to "Submitted"
    
    ✅ You'll receive a confirmation email
    
    ✅ Your approver will be notified
  </Step>
</Steps>

<img
  className="block"
  src="/images/help-center/expense-reports/web-submit-button.png"
  alt="Submit button location"
/>

<Check>
  **Success!** Your expense report has been submitted for approval.
</Check>

## What Happens After Submission

### Immediate Effects

When you submit a report:

1. **Status Changes**
   - Report status: Draft → Submitted
   - Expenses locked (cannot edit)
   - Report appears in "Submitted" section

2. **Notifications Sent**
   - Approver receives email notification
   - Approver gets push notification (if mobile app installed)
   - You receive submission confirmation email

3. **Approval Workflow Begins**
   - Report enters approval queue
   - First approver in chain is notified
   - Timeline entry created

<img
  className="block"
  src="/images/help-center/expense-reports/submission-flow.png"
  alt="Submission workflow"
/>

### Approval Process

Your report will go through these stages:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Single Approver">
    **Simple workflow:**
    
    1. **Submitted** - Waiting for manager review
    2. **Under Review** - Manager is reviewing
    3. **Approved** or **Rejected** - Final decision
    4. **Reimbursed** - Payment processed (if approved)
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Multi-Level Approval">
    **Complex workflow:**
    
    1. **Submitted** - Waiting for first approver
    2. **Level 1 Approved** - Sent to next approver
    3. **Level 2 Review** - Second approver reviewing
    4. **Final Approval** - All approvers approved
    5. **Finance Review** - Accounting team review
    6. **Approved** - Ready for reimbursement
    7. **Reimbursed** - Payment processed
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Conditional Approval">
    **Amount-based workflow:**
    
    **Under $500:**
    - Manager approval only
    - Typically 1-2 days
    
    **$500 - $2,000:**
    - Manager + Department Head
    - Typically 3-5 days
    
    **Over $2,000:**
    - Manager + Department Head + Finance
    - Typically 5-10 days
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Track Your Report Status

### On Web

1. Go to **Reports** → **Submitted**
2. Find your report in the list
3. Click to view details
4. Check the **Status** badge
5. View **Timeline** tab for approval progress

**Status indicators:**
- 🟡 **Submitted** - Waiting for review
- 🔵 **Under Review** - Being reviewed
- 🟢 **Approved** - Approved, awaiting reimbursement
- 🔴 **Rejected** - Returned for changes
- 💰 **Reimbursed** - Payment processed

1. Open **Reports** tab

## Submission Deadline

### Company Deadlines

Many companies have submission deadlines:

**Common deadlines:**
- **Monthly**: Submit by 5th of following month
- **Bi-weekly**: Submit within 2 weeks of expense date
- **Quarterly**: Submit by end of quarter + 10 days

**Check your deadline:**
1. Go to Settings → Business Settings
2. View **Expense Policy**
3. Check **Submission Deadline** section

<Warning>
  **Late submissions**: Expenses submitted after the deadline may not be reimbursed or may require special approval.
</Warning>

### Deadline Reminders

Smartbills can remind you:

1. **Email reminders** - 3 days before deadline
2. **Push notifications** - 1 day before deadline
3. **Dashboard alerts** - On deadline day

**Enable reminders:**
- Web: Settings → Notifications → Expense Reminders
- Mobile: Settings → Notifications → Report Deadlines

## Submission Errors & Warnings

### Common Errors

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Missing Required Fields" icon="circle-exclamation">
    **Error**: "Cannot submit - required fields missing"
    
    **What to check:**
    - All expenses have categories
    - Business purpose is documented
    - Custom required fields are filled
    - Payment method is selected
    
    **Solution:**
    1. Review each expense
    2. Fill in missing information
    3. Save changes
    4. Try submitting again
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Missing Receipts" icon="image">
    **Error**: "Receipts required for expenses over $X"
    
    **Company policies often require receipts for:**
    - All expenses (some companies)
    - Expenses over $25
    - Expenses over $75
    - Specific categories (meals, travel, etc.)
    
    **Solution:**
    1. Identify expenses without receipts
    2. Upload receipt images
    3. If receipt is lost, add explanation note
    4. Request manager override if needed
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Policy Violations" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    **Warning**: "This report contains policy violations"
    
    **Common violations:**
    - Amount exceeds category limit
    - Expense outside allowed date range
    - Unapproved category used
    - Missing pre-approval for large expense
    
    **What to do:**
    1. Review flagged expenses
    2. Add explanatory notes
    3. Attach supporting documentation
    4. Submit anyway (manager will review)
    5. Or remove violating expenses
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Duplicate Expenses" icon="copy">
    **Warning**: "Possible duplicate expenses detected"
    
    **Smartbills checks for:**
    - Same merchant and amount
    - Same date
    - Similar receipt images
    
    **Solution:**
    1. Review flagged expenses
    2. Remove actual duplicates
    3. Add notes if they're legitimate separate expenses
    4. Confirm and submit
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Report Too Old" icon="calendar-xmark">
    **Error**: "Expenses are outside the allowed date range"
    
    **Reason**: Company policy limits how old expenses can be
    
    **Solution:**
    1. Check company policy for date limits
    2. Remove expenses outside the range
    3. Request exception from manager
    4. Submit a separate report with explanation
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## After Submission Actions

### What You Can Do

After submitting, you can:

- ✅ View report details
- ✅ Check approval status
- ✅ View timeline
- ✅ Add comments
- ✅ Recall report (if needed)
- ✅ Download PDF copy

### What You Cannot Do

After submitting, you cannot:

- ❌ Edit expenses
- ❌ Add new expenses
- ❌ Remove expenses
- ❌ Change report details
- ❌ Delete the report

<Note>
  **Need to make changes?** You must recall the report first. See [Recall a Report](/help-center/expense-reports/recall-report).
</Note>

## Recall vs. Reject

### Recall (You initiate)

**When to recall:**
- You found an error
- Need to add more expenses
- Want to change something
- Forgot to include an expense

**How to recall:**
1. Open the submitted report
2. Click **Recall Report**
3. Add reason (optional)
4. Confirm recall
5. Make your changes
6. Resubmit when ready

See: [Recall a Report](/help-center/expense-reports/recall-report)

### Reject (Manager initiates)

**Manager may reject if:**
- Policy violations
- Missing information
- Incorrect amounts
- Need clarification

**When rejected:**
1. You'll receive notification
2. Report returns to draft status
3. Manager's comments explain why
4. Make requested changes
5. Resubmit the report

See: [Handle Rejected Reports](/help-center/expense-reports/reject-report)

## Tips for Faster Approval

<Steps>
  <Step title="Submit Early">
    Don't wait until the deadline. Submit as soon as expenses are complete.
    
    **Benefits:**
    - Faster reimbursement
    - Less rush for approvers
    - Time to address issues
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Provide Context">
    Add clear notes explaining:
    - Business purpose
    - Why amounts are high
    - Any unusual circumstances
    - Project or client details
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Follow Company Policies">
    - Stay within spending limits
    - Use approved categories
    - Get pre-approval when required
    - Submit within deadline
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Keep Receipts Clear">
    - High-quality images
    - All information visible
    - Multiple pages if needed
    - Straight and well-lit
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Categorize Correctly">
    - Use specific categories
    - Don't use "Other" unless necessary
    - Match company chart of accounts
    - Be consistent
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Communicate">
    If you have unusual expenses:
    - Email your manager beforehand
    - Explain in report notes
    - Attach supporting documents
    - Be proactive
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Bulk Submission

### Submit Multiple Reports

If you have several reports ready:

**Web:**
1. Go to **Reports** → **Drafts**
2. Select multiple reports (checkboxes)
3. Click **Bulk Actions** → **Submit Selected**
4. Review summary
5. Click **Submit All**

**Mobile:**
- Submit reports individually
- Bulk submission not available on mobile

<Tip>
  **Organize first**: Group expenses by project, month, or purpose before creating multiple reports.
</Tip>
