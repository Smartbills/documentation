---
title: "Reject an Expense Report"
description: "Return an expense report to the employee with feedback"
---

## Overview

Rejecting an expense report sends it back to the employee with your feedback, allowing them to make corrections and resubmit.

## Web Dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Report">
    Go to **Expense Report Approvals** and click on the submitted report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Issues">
    Identify the problems — missing receipts, incorrect categories, unauthorized expenses, etc.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Reject">
    Click the **Reject** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provide a Reason">
    Enter a clear explanation of why the report is being rejected and what the employee needs to fix. Be specific.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Confirm Rejection**. The employee receives a notification with your feedback.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Happens After Rejection

- The report status changes to **Rejected**
- The employee receives a notification with the rejection reason
- The employee can edit the report and resubmit it
- The report goes back through the approval workflow

<Tip>
If only some expenses are problematic, consider using **Request Changes** instead of rejecting the entire report.
</Tip>
