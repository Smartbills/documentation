## Overview

Export expense reports for accounting, record-keeping, or to share with external stakeholders.

## Web Dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Report">
    Navigate to **My Expense Reports** or **Expense Report Approvals** and open the report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Export">
    Click the **Export** button in the report toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Format">
    Select your preferred format:
    - **PDF** — Formatted report with expense details
    - **CSV** — Spreadsheet-compatible data export
    - **ZIP** — All receipt attachments bundled together
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download">
    The file downloads to your computer automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Bulk Export

To export multiple reports:

1. Go to **My Expense Reports**
2. Select multiple reports using checkboxes
3. Click **Export** in the bulk action toolbar
4. Choose your format

## What's Included

| Format | Includes |
|--------|----------|
| **PDF** | Report summary, expense list, amounts, categories, notes |
| **CSV** | All expense data fields in tabular format |
| **ZIP** | Receipt images and documents for all expenses |

<Tip>
CSV exports are ideal for importing into accounting software like QuickBooks or Xero.
</Tip>
