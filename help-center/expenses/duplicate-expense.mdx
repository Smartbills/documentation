---
title: "Duplicate an Expense"
description: "Copy an existing expense to quickly create similar entries"
---

## Overview

Duplicating an expense creates a copy with the same vendor, category, and details, saving time for recurring purchases.

## Web Dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Expense">
    Navigate to your expense list and click on the expense you want to duplicate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Actions Menu">
    Click the **three-dot menu** (⋯) in the top-right corner of the expense detail view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Duplicate">
    Click **Duplicate**. A new expense is created with the same vendor, category, and notes. The date is set to today.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit as Needed">
    Update the amount, date, or any other details that differ from the original.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Duplicating is ideal for recurring expenses like weekly office lunches or monthly subscriptions.
</Tip>
