## Overview

The Smartbills mobile app uses OCR (optical character recognition) to automatically extract vendor, date, amount, taxes, and line items from scanned receipts.

## Scanning a Receipt

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Scanner">
    Tap the **Upload** button (camera icon) in the center of the bottom navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Position the Receipt">
    Hold your phone steady and align the receipt within the camera frame. For best results:
    - Place the receipt on a flat, dark surface
    - Ensure good lighting — avoid shadows
    - Capture the full receipt including the top (vendor name) and bottom (total)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Capture">
    Tap the capture button. You can also select an existing photo from your gallery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Processing">
    The app processes the receipt and extracts:
    - **Vendor name** and address
    - **Date** of purchase
    - **Total amount** and currency
    - **Tax amounts**
    - **Individual line items** (when available)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify and Save">
    Review the extracted data and correct any errors. Assign a category and add notes if needed, then tap **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips for Better Scans

- **Flatten the receipt** — Smooth out creases and folds
- **Use good lighting** — Natural light or well-lit rooms work best
- **Avoid glare** — Tilt the receipt slightly if the camera flash creates glare
- **Capture the full receipt** — Make sure all text is visible, especially totals
- **One receipt per scan** — Scan each receipt individually for best accuracy

## Supported Receipt Types

| Type | Support |
|------|---------|
| Printed thermal receipts | Full support |
| Paper receipts / invoices | Full support |
| Digital receipts (screenshots) | Supported via gallery upload |
| Handwritten receipts | Limited support |

## Multi-Page Receipts

For long receipts that don't fit in a single photo:

1. Take multiple photos capturing overlapping sections
2. Upload all photos for the same expense
3. The OCR engine will combine data from all images

<Tip>
Enable **Auto-Capture** in settings for hands-free scanning — the app will automatically take the photo when a receipt is detected and in focus.
</Tip>
