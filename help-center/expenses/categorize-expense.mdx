## Overview

Proper categorization is essential for accurate expense tracking, tax compliance, and financial reporting. SmartBills makes it easy to organize expenses with categories, subcategories, and tags.

<Note>
  **Why categorize?** Proper categorization helps with tax deductions, budget tracking, and financial reporting. It's also often required by company policy.
</Note>

## Understanding Categories

### What are Categories?

Categories are predefined expense types that help organize and classify your spending. Common categories include:

- **Travel** - Airfare, lodging, rental cars
- **Meals & Entertainment** - Client dinners, team lunches
- **Office Supplies** - Pens, paper, equipment
- **Software & Subscriptions** - SaaS tools, licenses
- **Professional Services** - Consultants, legal fees
- **Marketing** - Advertising, promotional materials
- **Utilities** - Internet, phone, electricity

### Category Hierarchy

Many categories have subcategories for more specific classification:

```
Travel
├── Airfare
├── Lodging
├── Ground Transportation
│   ├── Rental Car
│   ├── Taxi/Rideshare
│   └── Parking
└── Mileage
```

## Categorizing on Web

### Assign Category to Single Expense

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Expense">
    1. Navigate to **Expenses** in sidebar
    2. Click on the expense you want to categorize
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Select Category">
    1. Click the **Category** dropdown
    2. Browse or search for the category
    3. Select the appropriate category
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Choose Subcategory (if applicable)">
    1. If the category has subcategories, a second dropdown appears
    2. Select the most specific subcategory
    3. Example: Travel → Lodging
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Save">
    1. Click **Save** or press Enter
    2. Category is applied immediately
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Quick Categorize (Inline)

For faster categorization:

1. **Hover over expense** in the list
2. **Click the category field**
3. **Select from dropdown**
4. **Press Enter** to save

No need to open the full expense details!

### Bulk Categorize

Categorize multiple expenses at once:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Expenses">
    1. Check boxes next to expenses
    2. Or use **Select All** checkbox
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Bulk Actions">
    1. Click **Bulk Actions** button
    2. Select **Change Category**
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Choose Category">
    1. Select the category from dropdown
    2. Choose subcategory if needed
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Apply">
    1. Click **Apply to Selected**
    2. All selected expenses are updated
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  **Time saver**: Use bulk categorize for similar expenses like multiple taxi rides or coffee purchases.
</Tip>

## Smart Categorization

### Auto-Categorization

SmartBills can automatically suggest categories based on:

- **Merchant name** - "Starbucks" → Meals & Entertainment
- **Past history** - You always categorize "Office Depot" as Office Supplies
- **Amount patterns** - Small amounts from coffee shops → Meals
- **AI learning** - System learns from your categorization patterns

**Enable auto-categorization:**
1. Go to Settings → Expenses
2. Enable **Auto-Categorize**
3. Choose confidence level (High, Medium, Low)

<Tip>
  **Review suggestions**: Always review auto-categorized expenses before submitting reports.
</Tip>

### Category Rules

Create rules for automatic categorization:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Rule">
    1. Go to Settings → Expenses → **Category Rules**
    2. Click **Add Rule**
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Define Conditions">
    Set conditions like:
    - Merchant contains "Starbucks"
    - Amount less than $50
    - Payment method is "Corporate Card"
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Set Category">
    1. Choose the category to apply
    2. Select subcategory if needed
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Save Rule">
    1. Name your rule
    2. Click **Save**
    3. Rule applies to future expenses
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Example rules:**
- All Uber/Lyft → Travel → Ground Transportation → Taxi/Rideshare
- Amazon under $100 → Office Supplies
- Hotel expenses → Travel → Lodging

## Using Tags

### What are Tags?

Tags are flexible labels you can add to expenses for additional organization. Unlike categories (which are predefined), you can create custom tags.

**Common uses:**
- **Projects**: "Project Alpha", "Website Redesign"
- **Clients**: "Acme Corp", "Client XYZ"
- **Locations**: "NYC Office", "Remote"
- **Status**: "Billable", "Urgent", "Reimbursable"
- **Events**: "Conference 2024", "Team Offsite"

### Adding Tags

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Web">
    1. Open the expense
    2. Click **Tags** field
    3. Start typing tag name
    4. Select existing tag or create new
    5. Add multiple tags if needed
    6. Click **Save**
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Mobile">
    1. Open expense
    2. Tap **Tags**
    3. Search or browse existing tags
    4. Tap to add
    5. Or tap **Create New Tag**
    6. Tap **Done**
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Tag Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Use Consistent Naming" icon="tags">
    **Do:**
    - "Q1-2024" (not "Q1 2024" or "q1-24")
    - "Client-Acme" (not "Acme Client" or "ACME")
    - "Billable" (not "bill" or "to-bill")
    
    **Why:** Consistency makes filtering and reporting easier
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Create a Tag System" icon="sitemap">
    Establish tag categories:
    - **Project tags**: Project-Alpha, Project-Beta
    - **Client tags**: Client-Acme, Client-XYZ
    - **Status tags**: Billable, Non-Billable, Urgent
    - **Location tags**: NYC, SF, Remote
    
    **Document your system** so team members use tags consistently
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Don't Over-Tag" icon="ban">
    **Avoid:**
    - Adding too many tags to one expense
    - Creating tags that duplicate category information
    - Using tags for information that belongs in other fields
    
    **Keep it simple:** 2-3 relevant tags per expense is usually enough
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Clean Up Unused Tags" icon="broom">
    Periodically:
    - Review tag list
    - Merge similar tags
    - Delete unused tags
    - Rename for clarity
    
    **Access:** Settings → Expenses → Manage Tags
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Category Management

### View All Categories

**Web:**
1. Go to Settings → Expenses → **Categories**
2. View complete category list
3. See hierarchy and subcategories

**Mobile:**
1. Settings → Business Settings → **Categories**
2. Browse category list

### Request New Category

If you need a category that doesn't exist:

1. **Contact your administrator**
2. Explain why you need the category
3. Suggest where it fits in the hierarchy
4. Admin can add it in Settings

<Note>
  **Admin only**: Only business administrators can add, edit, or delete categories.
</Note>

### Custom Categories (Admin)

Administrators can create custom categories:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Category Settings">
    Settings → Expenses → **Categories** → **Add Category**
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Enter Details">
    - Category name
    - Parent category (if subcategory)
    - Description
    - Tax treatment
    - GL code (if applicable)
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Set Permissions">
    - Who can use this category
    - Approval requirements
    - Spending limits
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Save">
    Category is immediately available to users
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tax Categories

### Understanding Tax Treatment

Some categories have specific tax implications:

| Category | Tax Treatment | Notes |
|----------|---------------|-------|
| Meals & Entertainment | 50% deductible | IRS limitation |
| Travel | Fully deductible | If business purpose |
| Office Supplies | Fully deductible | Ordinary & necessary |
| Personal | Not deductible | Should be reimbursed |

<Warning>
  **Consult your accountant**: Tax rules vary by jurisdiction and business type. Always verify with your tax professional.
</Warning>

### Tax Category Assignment

Your administrator can set tax treatment for each category:

1. Settings → Expenses → Categories
2. Edit category
3. Set **Tax Treatment**:
   - Fully deductible
   - Partially deductible (specify %)
   - Not deductible
   - Varies (case by case)

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can't find the right category" icon="magnifying-glass">
    **Try these:**
    
    1. **Use search** - Type keywords in category search
    2. **Check subcategories** - Category might be nested
    3. **Use "Other"** - Temporarily, then ask admin to add proper category
    4. **Add notes** - Explain what the expense is for
    
    **Request new category** if you frequently can't find the right one
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Category won't save" icon="circle-exclamation">
    **Possible reasons:**
    
    1. **Required field** - Some categories require additional info
    2. **Permission issue** - You might not have access to that category
    3. **Network problem** - Check internet connection
    4. **Validation error** - Check for error messages
    
    **Solution:** Try a different category or contact support
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Wrong category applied" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    **If auto-categorization is wrong:**
    
    1. **Manually correct** the category
    2. **Disable auto-categorize** for that merchant
    3. **Create a rule** for correct categorization
    4. **Report issue** to help improve AI
    
    **Settings:** Settings → Expenses → Auto-Categorization
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Tags not appearing" icon="tags">
    **Check these:**
    
    1. **Spelling** - Tags are case-sensitive
    2. **Permissions** - Some tags might be restricted
    3. **Sync issue** - Refresh the page/app
    4. **Deleted tag** - Admin might have removed it
    
    **Create new tag** if it doesn't exist
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Best Practices

### For Employees

<Steps>
  <Step title="Categorize Immediately">
    Categorize expenses as soon as you upload them. Don't wait until report time.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Be Specific">
    Use the most specific subcategory available. "Travel → Lodging" is better than just "Travel".
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Add Context">
    Use tags and notes to add context that categories alone can't provide.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Follow Company Policy">
    Use categories as defined by your company policy. Don't create your own interpretation.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Review Before Submitting">
    Double-check all categories before submitting expense reports.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### For Administrators

<Steps>
  <Step title="Keep It Simple">
    Don't create too many categories. 15-25 main categories is usually sufficient.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Document Your System">
    Create a guide explaining:
    - What each category is for
    - When to use subcategories
    - Examples for each category
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Align with Accounting">
    Match categories to your chart of accounts for easier reconciliation.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Review Regularly">
    Quarterly review:
    - Are categories being used correctly?
    - Do you need new categories?
    - Can any be merged or removed?
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Train Your Team">
    Provide training on:
    - How to categorize
    - Why it matters
    - Common mistakes to avoid
  </Step>
</Steps>
