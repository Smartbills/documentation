---

## Overview

Splitting an expense lets you allocate portions of a single receipt to different categories, departments, or cost centers.

## Web Dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Expense">
    Navigate to **Expenses** or **Wallet** and click on the expense you want to split.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Split">
    In the expense detail view, click the **Split** button in the toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define Splits">
    Add split lines and configure each one:
    - **Amount** — The dollar amount for this portion
    - **Category** — Assign a category to this split
    - **Note** — Optional description for context
    
    The total of all splits must equal the original expense amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Save Split** to create the individual expense items. The original expense will be replaced by the split items.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Mobile App

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Expense">
    Tap on an expense from your expense list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Split">
    Tap the **Split** option from the action menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Split Lines">
    Add amounts and categories for each portion of the expense.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Tap **Confirm Split** to finalize.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Splits are useful for meals with business and personal portions, or shared office supplies across departments.
</Tip>
