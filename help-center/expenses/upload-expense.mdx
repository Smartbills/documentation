## Overview

Smartbills makes it easy to upload expenses from receipts, invoices, or manual entry. Our intelligent OCR technology automatically extracts merchant names, amounts, dates, and categories from your receipts.

<Note>
  **Supported formats**: JPG, PNG, PDF, HEIC (max 10MB per file)
</Note>

## Web Application

### Method 1: Drag and Drop

The quickest way to upload expenses on the web:

1. Log in to your [Smartbills dashboard](https://app.smartbills.io)
2. Navigate to **Expenses**
3. Drag and drop receipt images directly onto the page
4. Smartbills will automatically process each receipt

<img
  className="block"
  src="/images/help-center/expenses/web-drag-drop.png"
  alt="Drag and drop upload"
/>

<Tip>
  You can upload multiple receipts at once! Just select multiple files or drag them all together.
</Tip>

### Method 2: Click to Upload

1. Click the **Upload Expense** button (or **+ New Expense**)
2. Click **Choose File** or **Browse**
3. Select one or more receipt images from your computer
4. Click **Open**
5. Review the extracted information
6. Click **Save**

<img
  className="block"
  src="/images/help-center/expenses/web-upload-button.png"
  alt="Upload button"
/>

### Method 3: Email Forwarding

Forward receipts directly to Smartbills:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Email Forwarding**
2. Copy your unique forwarding email address
3. Forward receipts to that address
4. Smartbills will automatically create expenses

<img
  className="block"
  src="/images/help-center/expenses/email-forwarding.png"
  alt="Email forwarding setup"
/>

<Check>
  **Pro tip**: Save your Smartbills forwarding email as a contact for quick access!
</Check>

## Mobile App (iOS & Android)

### Method 1: Scan Receipt (Recommended)

The fastest way to capture expenses on mobile:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iOS">
    1. Open the Smartbills app
    2. Tap the **+** button (bottom center)
    3. Tap **Scan Receipt**
    4. Point your camera at the receipt
    5. Align the receipt within the frame
    6. The app will automatically capture when ready
    7. Review and adjust the crop if needed
    8. Tap **Use Photo**
    9. Review extracted data
    10. Tap **Save**
    
    <img
      className="block"
      src="/images/help-center/expenses/ios-scan-receipt.png"
      alt="iOS receipt scanning"
    />
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Android">
    1. Open the Smartbills app
    2. Tap the **+** (FAB button)
    3. Tap **Scan Receipt**
    4. Point your camera at the receipt
    5. Align the receipt within the guides
    6. Tap the capture button or wait for auto-capture
    7. Adjust the corners if needed
    8. Tap **✓** to confirm
    9. Review extracted information
    10. Tap **Save Expense**
    
    <img
      className="block"
      src="/images/help-center/expenses/android-scan-receipt.png"
      alt="Android receipt scanning"
    />
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  **Pro tips for better scanning:**
  - Use good lighting
  - Flatten the receipt
  - Avoid shadows
  - Ensure all text is visible
  - Clean your camera lens
</Tip>

### Method 2: Upload from Photo Library

Upload receipts you've already photographed:

1. Tap the **+** button
2. Tap **Upload Photo**
3. Select **Photo Library**
4. Choose one or more receipt photos
5. Tap **Add** or **Done**
6. Review the extracted information
7. Tap **Save**

### Method 3: Manual Entry

Create an expense without a receipt:

1. Tap the **+** button
2. Tap **Manual Entry**
3. Enter expense details:
   - **Merchant name**

## After Upload: Review & Edit

### What Smartbills Extracts

Our OCR technology automatically identifies:

- ✅ Merchant name
- ✅ Total amount
- ✅ Transaction date
- ✅ Currency
- ✅ Tax amounts (when visible)
- ✅ Line items (on detailed receipts)
- ✅ Payment method (when visible)

<img
  className="block"
  src="/images/help-center/expenses/ocr-extraction.png"
  alt="OCR data extraction"
/>

### Review Extracted Data

Always verify the extracted information:

1. **Merchant**: Check spelling and accuracy
2. **Amount**: Verify the correct total
3. **Date**: Confirm the transaction date
4. **Category**: Select or confirm the category
5. **Tax**: Verify tax amounts if applicable

<Warning>
  **Important**: OCR is highly accurate but not perfect. Always review extracted data before saving.
</Warning>

### Edit if Needed

To correct any extracted information:

<Tabs>

  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Mobile">
    1. Tap any field to edit
    2. Enter the correct information
    3. Tap **Done** on keyboard
    4. Tap **Save Expense**
  </Tab>

## Tips for Best Results

### Receipt Photography Tips

<Steps>
  <Step title="Use Good Lighting">
    Natural light or bright indoor lighting works best. Avoid dim environments.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Flatten the Receipt">
    Smooth out wrinkles and folds for better OCR accuracy.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Avoid Glare">
    Don't use flash directly on shiny thermal receipts. Angle slightly to avoid glare.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Fill the Frame">
    Get close enough so the receipt fills most of the frame, but don't cut off edges.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Capture Immediately">
    Scan receipts right away before they fade (especially thermal receipts).
  </Step>
</Steps>

### File Organization

- **Name files descriptively**: `2024-01-15_office_depot_supplies.jpg`
- **Keep originals**: Smartbills stores images, but keep physical receipts per company policy
- **Use folders**: Organize receipts by month or project before uploading

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="OCR isn't extracting data correctly" icon="eye-slash">
    **Try these solutions:**
    1. Ensure the receipt is clear and readable
    2. Retake the photo with better lighting
    3. Make sure all text is visible in the image
    4. Try straightening a crooked receipt
    5. For faded receipts, use manual entry instead
    
    **Common issues:**
    - Crumpled or folded receipts
    - Faded thermal paper
    - Poor lighting or shadows
    - Glare on the receipt
    - Receipt is too small in the photo
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Upload is stuck or failing" icon="circle-exclamation">
    **On Web:**
    1. Check your internet connection
    2. Try a different browser
    3. Clear browser cache
    4. Disable browser extensions
    5. Reduce file size if image is very large
    
    **On Mobile:**
    1. Check your internet connection
    2. Close and reopen the app
    3. Update to the latest app version
    4. Check available storage space
    5. Try uploading one file at a time
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="File size too large" icon="file">
    If your file exceeds 10MB:
    
    1. **Resize the image** using your phone's built-in tools
    2. **Use a compression app** to reduce file size
    3. **Take a new photo** with lower resolution
    4. **For PDFs**: Split multi-page PDFs into individual pages
    
    Smartbills accepts files up to 10MB each.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Wrong currency detected" icon="dollar-sign">
    If OCR detects the wrong currency:
    
    1. Click/tap the currency field
    2. Select the correct currency from the dropdown
    3. Smartbills will remember your preference
    4. Set your default currency in Settings to prevent this
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can't find uploaded expense" icon="magnifying-glass">
    If an expense seems to be missing:
    
    1. Check your **Filters** - you might have filters applied
    2. Look in **All Expenses** tab
    3. Check if it was assigned to a different business
    4. Use the Search function to find by merchant or amount
    5. Check the date range filter
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Advanced Features

### Receipt Matching

Smartbills can match receipts with imported transactions:

1. Upload your receipt
2. If a matching bank transaction exists, Smartbills will suggest it
3. Click **Match** to link them
4. This provides complete documentation

<img
  className="block"
  src="/images/help-center/expenses/receipt-matching.png"
  alt="Receipt matching"
/>

### Smart Categories

Smartbills learns from your categorization:

- Frequently used categories appear first
- AI suggests categories based on merchant
- Create custom categories for your needs
- Bulk recategorize similar expenses

### Duplicate Detection

Smartbills alerts you to potential duplicates:

1. When uploading, Smartbills checks for similar expenses
2. If found, you'll see a warning
3. Review the potential duplicate
4. Confirm if it's a duplicate or a separate expense
