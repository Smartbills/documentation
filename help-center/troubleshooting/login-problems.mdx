## Common Issues

### Can't Sign In

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Incorrect email or password">
    - Double-check your email address for typos
    - Passwords are case-sensitive
    - Try [resetting your password](/help-center/account/reset-password)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Account locked">
    After multiple failed login attempts, your account may be temporarily locked for security. Wait 15 minutes and try again, or contact support.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Email not found">
    - Verify you're using the email address you registered with
    - Check if you signed up with a different email or via SSO
    - Contact support if you can't locate your account
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="SSO not working">
    If your company uses Single Sign-On:
    - Make sure you're clicking the SSO button, not entering credentials manually
    - Contact your IT administrator to verify your SSO configuration
    - Clear your browser cookies and try again
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Password Reset Not Working

- Check your spam/junk folder for the reset email
- The reset link expires after 24 hours — request a new one
- Make sure you're checking the correct email inbox

### Mobile App Login Issues

- Update the app to the latest version
- Clear the app cache (Settings → Apps → Smartbills → Clear Cache)
- Try uninstalling and reinstalling the app
- Check your internet connection

## Still Having Issues?

Contact us at [support@smartbills.io](mailto:support@smartbills.io) with:
- Your email address
- The device and browser/app version you're using
- A screenshot of any error message
