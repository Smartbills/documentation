## General

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Smartbills?">
    Smartbills is an expense management platform that helps individuals and businesses track, organize, and report on expenses. Upload receipts, let AI-powered OCR extract the details, categorize spending, create expense reports, and submit them for approval, all from web, iOS, or Android.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How much does Smartbills cost?">
    Smartbills offers several plans:

    - **Free**: For individuals tracking personal expenses. Includes basic receipt scanning and expense tracking.
    - **Pro**: For professionals and small teams. Includes advanced OCR, unlimited uploads, expense reports, and priority support.
    - **Business**: For growing organizations. Includes team management, approval workflows, integrations, and dedicated support.
    - **Enterprise**: For large organizations with custom requirements. Includes SSO, advanced security, custom integrations, and a dedicated account manager.

    Visit [smartbills.io/pricing](https://smartbills.io/pricing) for current pricing details and to compare plans.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my data secure?">
    Yes. Smartbills takes data security seriously:

    - **Encryption in transit**: All data is encrypted using TLS 1.2+ during transmission
    - **Encryption at rest**: Data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption
    - **Cloud infrastructure**: Hosted on enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure with SOC 2 compliance
    - **Access controls**: Role-based access controls ensure users only see data they are authorized to view
    - **Two-factor authentication**: Optional 2FA for all accounts, with authenticator app and SMS support
    - **Regular audits**: Security practices are regularly audited and updated
    - **Data residency**: Data is stored in compliance with applicable regulations

    For more details, review our [Security Policy](https://smartbills.io/security) and [Privacy Policy](https://smartbills.io/privacy).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What platforms is Smartbills available on?">
    Smartbills is available on:

    - **Web application**: [app.smartbills.io](https://app.smartbills.io), works on any modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
    - **iOS app**: Requires iOS 14.0 or later. [Download from the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/smartbills)
    - **Android app**: Requires Android 8.0 or later. [Download from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.smartbills.app)
    - **Outlook Add-in**: Forward email receipts directly to Smartbills. [Learn more](/integrations/outlook)
    - **SDKs**: Build custom integrations with the [Smartbills client libraries](/sdks/overview)

    All platforms sync data in real time, so you can start on one device and continue on another.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Expenses

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I add an expense?">
    There are several ways to add an expense:

    1. **Upload a receipt**: Drag and drop an image or PDF on web, or use the camera scanner on mobile. [Upload guide](/guides/web-app/expenses/upload-expense)
    2. **Manual entry**: Create an expense manually by entering the merchant, amount, date, and category
    3. **Email forwarding**: Forward receipt emails to your unique Smartbills email address
    4. **Outlook Add-in**: Use the Smartbills button in Outlook to send receipts directly

    On all platforms, navigate to **Expenses** and click or tap the **+** button to get started.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What file formats are supported for receipts?">
    Smartbills supports the following formats:

    - **Images**: JPG, JPEG, PNG, HEIC, WEBP (max 10 MB)
    - **Documents**: PDF (max 25 MB, up to 50 pages)

    Unsupported formats include Word documents, Excel files, GIF images, and SVG files. Convert these to PDF before uploading.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I split an expense across categories?">
    Yes. Use the split feature to divide a single expense across multiple categories, projects, or cost centers:

    1. Open the expense
    2. Click or tap **Split Expense**
    3. Add split lines with the amount and category for each
    4. Ensure the split amounts total the original expense amount
    5. Save the split

    [Learn more about splitting expenses](/guides/web-app/expenses/split-expense)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does OCR receipt scanning work?">
    When you upload a receipt image or PDF, Smartbills uses AI-powered Optical Character Recognition (OCR) to automatically extract key information:

    - **Merchant name**: the business or vendor
    - **Total amount**: the total charge
    - **Date**: the transaction date
    - **Currency**: detected from symbols or merchant location
    - **Tax amount**: sales tax or VAT if present
    - **Line items**: individual items on the receipt (when available)

    OCR processing typically takes 5 to 30 seconds depending on document complexity. You can always manually edit any extracted field if the OCR result is inaccurate.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if OCR extracts incorrect data?">
    OCR accuracy depends on image quality, receipt formatting, and text clarity. If extracted data is incorrect:

    1. Open the expense
    2. Tap or click on the incorrect field (merchant, amount, date, etc.)
    3. Manually enter the correct value
    4. Save the expense

    To improve OCR accuracy in the future, ensure receipts are well-lit, flat, in focus, and captured at a high resolution. See our [upload tips](/help-center/troubleshooting/upload-problems) for best practices.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Expense Reports

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I create an expense report?">
    To create an expense report:

    1. Navigate to **Expense Reports** in the sidebar (web) or bottom navigation (mobile)
    2. Click or tap **New Report**
    3. Enter a report name and description
    4. Add expenses to the report from your expense list
    5. Review the report total and details
    6. Submit the report for approval

    [Step-by-step report creation guide](/guides/web-app/reports/create-report)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does approval take?">
    Approval timelines depend on your organization's workflow:

    - **Single-level approval**: Your direct manager reviews and approves, typically within 1-3 business days
    - **Multi-level approval**: The report passes through multiple approvers in sequence. Each level adds processing time
    - **Auto-approval**: Some organizations configure automatic approval for reports below a certain threshold

    You can track the status of your submitted report in **Expense Reports** > select the report > view the **Approval Timeline**.

    <Tip>
      Enable push notifications to receive real-time updates when your report status changes.
    </Tip>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I recall a submitted report?">
    Yes, you can recall a report that has been submitted but not yet fully approved:

    1. Go to **Expense Reports**
    2. Select the submitted report
    3. Click or tap **Recall Report**
    4. Confirm the recall

    After recalling, the report returns to draft status and you can make changes before resubmitting.

    <Note>
      If a report has already been approved by one or more approvers in a multi-level workflow, recalling it will reset all approvals. The report will need to go through the full approval chain again.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I get reimbursed?">
    The reimbursement process depends on your organization's configuration:

    1. **Submit your expense report** and wait for approval
    2. **Report approved**: once all required approvers have approved the report, it moves to the reimbursement queue
    3. **Finance processes payment**: your finance team or administrator processes the reimbursement through their preferred method (direct deposit, check, payroll, or integrated payment provider)
    4. **Reimbursement completed**: you will receive a notification when the reimbursement is marked as complete

    Reimbursement timelines vary by organization. Contact your finance department or administrator for specific timelines.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Team and Permissions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I invite team members?">
    Administrators can invite team members:

    1. Go to **Settings** > **Users**
    2. Click **Invite User**
    3. Enter the team member's email address
    4. Select their role (Employee, Manager, Approver, Administrator)
    5. Assign them to a department (optional)
    6. Click **Send Invitation**

    The team member will receive an email invitation with a link to join your business. If they already have a Smartbills account, they can accept the invitation directly. New users will be guided through account creation.

    [Learn more about managing users](/guides/web-app/team/invite-members)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are the different user roles?">
    Smartbills provides role-based access control:

    | Role | Permissions |
    |---|---|
    | **Employee** | Create and manage personal expenses, submit expense reports |
    | **Manager** | All Employee permissions + view and manage team expenses |
    | **Approver** | All Manager permissions + approve or reject expense reports |
    | **Administrator** | Full access to all settings, users, integrations, and data |
    | **Owner** | All Administrator permissions + billing management and account deletion |

    [Learn more about roles and permissions](/guides/concepts/roles-and-permissions)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I delegate approval authority?">
    Yes. Administrators can set up delegation for approvers who are unavailable (vacation, leave, etc.):

    1. Go to **Settings** > **Approval Workflows**
    2. Select the approver who needs a delegate
    3. Click **Add Delegate**
    4. Choose the delegate user and set the delegation period (start and end dates)
    5. Save the delegation

    During the delegation period, the delegate will receive and process approval requests on behalf of the original approver. Both the original approver and the delegate can see the delegation status.

    [Learn more about approval workflows](/guides/concepts/approval-workflows)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Integrations

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What accounting software does Smartbills integrate with?">
    Smartbills integrates with popular accounting platforms:

    - **QuickBooks Online**: Sync expenses, categories, and tax codes. [Setup guide](/integrations/quickbooks)
    - **Xero**: Sync expenses, contacts, and chart of accounts. [Setup guide](/integrations/xero)
    - **Sage**: Export expenses and reports to Sage
    - **FreshBooks**: Connect for expense synchronization

    Additional integrations include:
    - **Slack**: Receive notifications and approve reports from Slack. [Setup guide](/integrations/slack)
    - **Zapier**: Connect Smartbills to 1000+ apps. [Setup guide](/integrations/zapier)
    - **Banking**: Link bank accounts for automatic transaction import. [Setup guide](/integrations/plaid)

    Enterprise customers can request custom integrations. Contact [sales@smartbills.io](mailto:sales@smartbills.io) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I set up the Outlook add-in?">
    The Smartbills Outlook add-in lets you forward receipt emails directly to Smartbills:

    1. Open Outlook (desktop or web)
    2. Go to **Get Add-ins** (Home tab > Get Add-ins, or Settings > Manage Add-ins)
    3. Search for "Smartbills"
    4. Click **Add** to install the add-in
    5. Sign in with your Smartbills credentials
    6. To forward a receipt, open the email and click the Smartbills icon in the toolbar

    The add-in extracts attachments and relevant information from the email, creating an expense in your Smartbills account automatically.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Smartbills work with Slack?">
    Yes. The Smartbills Slack integration provides:

    - **Notifications**: receive alerts when reports are submitted, approved, or rejected
    - **Quick approvals**: approve or reject expense reports directly from Slack
    - **Expense summaries**: get daily or weekly expense summaries in a Slack channel

    To set up: go to **Settings** > **Integrations** > **Slack** > **Connect** and authorize Smartbills in your Slack workspace.

    [Full Slack integration guide](/integrations/slack)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Billing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I change my plan?">
    To upgrade or downgrade your Smartbills plan:

    1. Go to **Settings** > **Billing** > **Plan**
    2. Review available plans and features
    3. Click **Change Plan** on your desired plan
    4. Confirm the change

    **Upgrades** take effect immediately. You will be charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the current billing cycle.

    **Downgrades** take effect at the end of your current billing cycle. You retain access to higher-tier features until then.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What payment methods are accepted?">
    Smartbills accepts the following payment methods:

    - **Credit cards**: Visa, Mastercard, American Express
    - **Debit cards**: Visa Debit, Mastercard Debit
    - **Bank transfer / ACH**: Available for annual Business and Enterprise plans

    All payments are processed securely through Stripe. Smartbills does not store your full card details.

    To update your payment method, go to **Settings** > **Billing** > **Payment Method**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I cancel my subscription?">
    To cancel your Smartbills subscription:

    1. Go to **Settings** > **Billing** > **Plan**
    2. Click **Cancel Subscription**
    3. Select a reason for cancellation (optional)
    4. Confirm the cancellation

    **What happens after cancellation:**
    - Your plan remains active until the end of the current billing period
    - After expiration, your account reverts to the free plan
    - Your data is retained, but access to premium features is removed
    - You can resubscribe at any time to regain access

    <Note>
      If you want to permanently delete your account and data, see [Delete Your Account](/help-center/account/delete-account). Cancelling a subscription does not delete your account.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
