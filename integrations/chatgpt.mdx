## Overview

ChatGPT connects to Smartbills through the same remote [MCP server](/developer/mcp) that
powers the Claude integration. Once connected, ChatGPT can search your expenses, reports,
receipts, vendors and spend policy, cite the records it used, and — with your confirmation —
file and submit expense reports.

## Key Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Connector search** | Ask about spend in plain language; ChatGPT queries Smartbills directly |
| **Deep research** | Smartbills appears as a research source with citations back to `app.smartbills.io` |
| **Report workflows** | Build, policy-check and submit expense reports |
| **Approvals** | Review what is waiting for your sign-off |
| **Read-only by default** | Every write action asks you to confirm first |

## Prerequisites

- A Smartbills account with access to at least one business
- A paid ChatGPT plan with connector support
- On Business and Enterprise workspaces, an admin must enable developer-mode connectors

## Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable developer mode">
    In ChatGPT, go to **Settings → Connectors → Advanced** and turn on **Developer mode**.
    (Once the Smartbills connector is published in the ChatGPT directory this step goes
    away.)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the connector">
    Choose **Create**, name it `Smartbills`, set the MCP server URL to
    `https://mcp.smartbills.io/mcp`, and select **OAuth** authentication. There is no client
    ID or secret to paste — Smartbills registers ChatGPT automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect**, sign in to Smartbills, choose the business ChatGPT may act on, and
    approve the requested permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use it in a chat">
    Open the **+ → Connectors** menu in a new chat and enable Smartbills, or select it as a
    source under **Deep research**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Menu names in ChatGPT change from time to time. If the wording differs, look for the
  connector or MCP server settings and enter the same URL.
</Note>

## Search and citations

For ChatGPT's research surfaces Smartbills exposes two extra read-only tools:

| Tool | Returns |
|---|---|
| `search` | Matching records across expenses, reports, receipts, vendors and policy, each with an id, title and citable URL |
| `fetch` | The full text of one record by id, with a link back to Smartbills |

Both respect your scopes and business membership exactly like every other tool — deep
research can read, but never change anything.

## Example prompts

- *"Using Smartbills, what were my top five vendors by spend last quarter?"*
- *"Which of my expenses from March are missing receipts?"*
- *"Summarise our expense policy for meals and travel."*
- *"Build an expense report from my unreported May expenses and check it against policy."*

## Billing

ChatGPT usage counts against your OpenAI plan. Smartbills does **not** charge AI tokens for
MCP tool calls.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The connector will not authenticate" icon="key">
    Confirm the URL ends in `/mcp` and that authentication is set to OAuth rather than an
    API key. Smartbills does not accept API keys over MCP.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Deep research does not see Smartbills" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Enable the connector as a source in the deep-research picker for that specific request;
    enabling it in Settings alone is not enough.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Fewer tools than expected" icon="wrench">
    Tools are filtered by your Smartbills scopes and your role in the selected business.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
