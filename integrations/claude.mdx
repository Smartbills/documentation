## Overview

Connect Claude to Smartbills and ask it to find expenses, summarise spend, check policy,
build an expense report, or review the approvals waiting for you. The connection is a
remote [MCP server](/developer/mcp) — nothing to install, and Claude only ever gets the
permissions your own Smartbills account already has.

## Key Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Natural-language search** | "What did we spend on travel in Q2?" across expenses, reports, receipts and vendors |
| **Report filing** | Gather the month's expenses into a report, run the policy check, and submit |
| **Approvals** | Review reports pending your approval with their violations summarised |
| **Policy answers** | Ask what needs a receipt or approval, answered from your live policy |
| **Confirmation on writes** | Claude never creates, submits, approves or rejects anything without asking you first |

## Prerequisites

- A Smartbills account with access to at least one business
- Claude on a Free, Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise plan (Free accounts are limited to one
  custom connector)
- For Team and Enterprise: an Owner adds the connector in organization settings first

## Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connectors">
    In Claude, go to **Customize → Connectors**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a custom connector">
    Click **+**, then **Add custom connector**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the Smartbills URL">
    Paste `https://mcp.smartbills.io/mcp` and click **Add**. Leave *Advanced settings*
    empty — Smartbills registers Claude automatically, so there is no client ID or secret
    to enter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect**, sign in to Smartbills, choose the business Claude may act on, and
    review the requested permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try it">
    Ask Claude: *"List my Smartbills businesses"*, then *"How much did we spend on software
    last quarter?"*
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  On Team and Enterprise plans, members find the connector already listed under
  **Customize → Connectors** and simply click **Connect** to authenticate with their own
  Smartbills account.
</Note>

## Claude Code

```bash
claude mcp add --transport http smartbills https://mcp.smartbills.io/mcp
```

Then run `/mcp` in the session to complete the sign-in.

## Claude Desktop without connectors

If your build of Claude Desktop has no connector UI, add a local proxy to
`claude_desktop_config.json` (macOS `~/Library/Application Support/Claude/`,
Windows `%APPDATA%\Claude\`) and restart Claude. Node.js 18+ is required.

```json
{
  "mcpServers": {
    "smartbills": {
      "command": "npx",
      "args": ["-y", "mcp-remote@latest", "https://mcp.smartbills.io/mcp", "--transport", "http-only"]
    }
  }
}
```

The proxy only relays traffic to the same remote server; the browser sign-in is identical.

## What Claude can do

Read tools cover expenses, expense reports, receipts, vendors, categories, employees,
policy, violations and spend summaries. Write tools cover creating and updating expenses,
building and submitting reports, uploading receipts, approving and rejecting reports, and
waiving violations. Money movement is not exposed at all. The full list is in the
[tool catalog](/developer/mcp#tool-catalog).

## Billing

Claude's usage counts against your Claude plan. Smartbills does **not** charge AI credits for
MCP tool calls.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Claude cannot reach the server" icon="plug">
    The URL must be exactly `https://mcp.smartbills.io/mcp`. Anthropic connects from its own
    IP ranges, so the server must be publicly reachable — that is the case for
    `mcp.smartbills.io`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Fewer tools than expected" icon="wrench">
    Tools are filtered by your scopes and your role in the selected business. Reconnect and
    review the consent screen.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Wrong business" icon="building">
    The business is chosen during sign-in and fixed for the connection. Disconnect,
    reconnect, and pick the other business.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
