## Overview

The Smartbills Outlook Add-in lets you capture receipts, forward expense emails, and upload documents directly from your Outlook inbox -- without switching to the Smartbills dashboard or mobile app.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/integrations/outlook/overview.png" alt="Smartbills Outlook Add-in sidebar" />
</Frame>

## Key Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Email Forwarding** | Forward receipt emails to Smartbills with one click |
| **File Upload** | Drag and drop receipt files directly from Outlook |
| **OCR Processing** | Automatic data extraction from receipts and invoices |
| **Business Selector** | Switch between multiple businesses |
| **Category Assignment** | Assign expense categories before creating |
| **Real-time Sync** | Expenses appear instantly in your Smartbills wallet |

## Prerequisites

- Microsoft Outlook (desktop or web version)
- A Smartbills account with an active business
- Admin permissions (for organization-wide deployment)

## Installation

### Individual Installation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Outlook">
    Launch Microsoft Outlook (desktop app or Outlook on the web).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access Add-ins">
    Click **Get Add-ins** from the ribbon (or **...** menu in Outlook Web).

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/integrations/outlook/get-addins.png" alt="Get Add-ins button in Outlook" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for Smartbills">
    In the Add-ins store, search for **Smartbills** and click **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign In">
    Click the Smartbills icon in the Outlook toolbar to open the sidebar. Sign in with your Smartbills credentials.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/integrations/outlook/sign-in.png" alt="Smartbills sign-in panel" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Business">
    If you belong to multiple businesses, select the one you want to use.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Organization-wide Deployment

Admins can deploy the Smartbills add-in for the entire organization via the Microsoft 365 Admin Center:

1. Go to **Microsoft 365 Admin Center** > **Settings** > **Integrated Apps**
2. Click **Upload custom apps** or search the store for **Smartbills**
3. Assign to all users or specific groups
4. Users will see the add-in automatically in their Outlook

## Forward Emails as Expenses

The primary use case -- forward a receipt email to Smartbills without leaving Outlook.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Email">
    Open any email that contains a receipt or invoice (either in the body or as an attachment).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Add-in">
    Click the **Smartbills** icon in the Outlook toolbar to open the sidebar panel.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/integrations/outlook/email-tab.png" alt="Email forwarding panel" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Extracted Data">
    Smartbills automatically detects attachments and extracts data from the email. Review the merchant, amount, and date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign Category">
    Select an expense category from the dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Expense">
    Click **Create Expense**. The expense appears immediately in your Smartbills wallet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Smartbills processes both email body content and attachments (PDF, JPG, PNG). If the email contains multiple receipts as attachments, each one is processed separately.
</Tip>

## Upload Files

Upload receipt files directly through the add-in without needing to forward an email.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Add-in">
    Click the Smartbills icon to open the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to Upload Tab">
    Click the **Upload** tab at the top of the panel.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/integrations/outlook/upload-tab.png" alt="File upload panel" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Files">
    Drag and drop files into the upload area, or click **Browse** to select files from your computer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Process and Save">
    OCR processes the uploaded files. Review the extracted data, assign a category, and click **Create Expense**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Configuration

### Switch Business

If you belong to multiple organizations, switch between them:

1. Click the **business name** at the top of the add-in panel
2. Select the desired business from the dropdown
3. All new expenses will be created under the selected business

### Settings

Access add-in settings by clicking the **gear icon** in the panel:

| Setting | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Default Category** | Pre-select a category for new expenses |
| **Auto-forward Rules** | Automatically process emails from specific senders |
| **Notifications** | Enable or disable processing notifications |
| **Language** | Switch between English and French |

## Supported File Formats

| Format | Max Size | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| JPG / JPEG | 10 MB | Receipt photos |
| PNG | 10 MB | Screenshots, scanned receipts |
| PDF | 25 MB | Invoices, statements |
| HEIC | 10 MB | iPhone photos |

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Add-in not appearing in Outlook">
    - Verify the add-in is installed: **File** > **Manage Add-ins**
    - Restart Outlook
    - For organization deployments, check with your IT admin
    - Ensure you're using a supported version of Outlook
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Unable to sign in">
    - Verify your Smartbills credentials at [app.smartbills.io](https://app.smartbills.io)
    - Clear the add-in cache: close and reopen the panel
    - Check that your organization allows third-party add-ins
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="OCR not extracting data correctly">
    - Ensure the receipt image is clear and well-lit
    - Try uploading a higher-resolution image
    - PDF invoices generally produce better results than photos
    - You can always edit the extracted data manually
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Expenses not appearing in dashboard">
    - Check your internet connection
    - Verify you selected the correct business
    - Refresh your Smartbills dashboard
    - Check the real-time sync indicator in the add-in panel
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
