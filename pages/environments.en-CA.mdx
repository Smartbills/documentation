# Environments  
Smartbills offers multiple environments so that you can develop and test your applications before deploying them in production.

## Sandbox Environment
The sandbox environment is identical to our production environment but is specifically designed for testing integrations.

- `https://sandbox-api.smartbills.io/auth`

- `https://sandbox-api.smartbills.io`

## Production Environment
When you have your production keys, you can modify your URLs to point to these addresses:

- `https://api.smartbills.io/auth`

- `https://api.smartbills.io`
