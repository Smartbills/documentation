## What is the Email Inbox?

The Email Inbox feature connects your email accounts to automatically import receipts and create expenses from emails.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Email">
    Link your Gmail, Outlook, or other email account
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan Inbox">
    Smartbills scans for receipt emails
  </Step>
  <Step title="Extract Data">
    AI extracts expense information
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Expense">
    Expenses created automatically
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    You review and approve imported expenses
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Features

### Automatic Detection

AI identifies receipt emails by:

- Subject line patterns
- Sender recognition
- Attachment analysis
- Email content parsing

### Smart Categorization

- Merchant recognition
- Category suggestion
- Duplicate detection
- Amount extraction

### Attachment Handling

- PDF invoices
- Image receipts
- HTML email receipts
- Multi-attachment emails

## Setting Up

### Connect Account

1. Go to **Settings > Email Accounts**
2. Click **Add Email Account**
3. Choose your provider
4. Sign in and authorize
5. Configure scan settings

### Configure Scanning

| Setting | Options |
|---------|---------|
| Scan Frequency | Real-time, hourly, daily |
| Folders | Inbox, specific folders |
| Senders | All, specific senders |
| Date Range | Last 30 days, custom |

## Processing Queue

### Review Imports

1. Go to **Inbox** in sidebar
2. View pending imports
3. Accept, edit, or reject
4. Accepted items become expenses

### Bulk Actions

- Accept all matching criteria
- Reject obvious non-receipts
- Categorize in bulk

## Privacy & Security

- OAuth 2.0 authentication
- Read-only access
- No password storage
- You control what's imported
