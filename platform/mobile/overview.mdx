## Smartbills Mobile

The Smartbills mobile app brings expense management to your pocket. Capture receipts, submit reports, and approve expenses from anywhere.

## Key Features

### Receipt Scanning

- Smart camera with edge detection
- AI-powered OCR
- Real-time data extraction
- Multi-page scanning

### Expense Management

- Create and edit expenses
- Categorize on-the-go
- Split expenses
- Add notes and descriptions

### Expense Reports

- Create reports
- Submit for approval
- Track status
- Respond to comments

### Approvals

- Review pending approvals
- Approve/reject from anywhere
- Add comments
- Push notifications

## Getting Started

### Sign In

1. Open the Smartbills app
2. Enter your email
3. Authenticate via SSO or password
4. Select your business

### Quick Capture

1. Tap the camera button
2. Point at receipt
3. Capture image
4. Review extracted data
5. Save expense

## Offline Mode

The app works offline:

- Capture receipts
- Create expenses
- Queue for upload
- Sync when connected

## Notifications

Receive push notifications for:

- Report approved/rejected
- Approval needed
- Comment on your report
- Reimbursement sent

## Tips

<Tip>
**Enable auto-upload** - Don't lose receipts if you lose your phone.
</Tip>

<Tip>
**Use good lighting** - Better photos mean better OCR.
</Tip>

<Tip>
**Categorize immediately** - Easier when you remember the expense.
</Tip>
