## What are Expenses?

Expenses in Smartbills represent individual business purchases that need to be tracked, categorized, and potentially reimbursed. Each expense contains information about the transaction including the amount, vendor, date, category, and supporting documentation (receipts).

## Expense Lifecycle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Capture">
    Upload a receipt image, forward an email, or manually enter expense details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Process">
    Smartbills extracts data using OCR and AI, including merchant, amount, and date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Categorize">
    Assign the expense to a category for proper accounting classification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    Verify extracted data and add any missing information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Report">
    Add the expense to an expense report for submission and reimbursement.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Expense Fields

| Field | Description | Required |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| **Merchant** | The vendor or business where the purchase was made | Yes |
| **Amount** | The total expense amount including taxes | Yes |
| **Date** | The transaction date | Yes |
| **Category** | Expense classification (e.g., Travel, Meals, Supplies) | Yes |
| **Currency** | The currency of the transaction | Yes |
| **Description** | Additional notes about the expense | No |
| **Receipt** | Supporting documentation (image or PDF) | Recommended |
| **Department** | The department to charge the expense to | No |
| **Project** | Associated project or cost center | No |
| **Payment Method** | How the expense was paid | No |

## Expense Status

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Draft** | Expense created but not yet added to a report |
| **Pending** | Waiting for processing or review |
| **In Report** | Added to an expense report |
| **Submitted** | Part of a submitted expense report |
| **Approved** | Expense has been approved |
| **Rejected** | Expense was rejected during approval |
| **Reimbursed** | Employee has been reimbursed |

## Ways to Add Expenses

### 1. Upload Receipt

The most common method - upload a photo or PDF of your receipt:

1. Click **Add Expense** in the dashboard
2. Drag and drop or select your receipt file
3. Wait for automatic data extraction
4. Review and confirm the extracted data
5. Add category and any missing information
6. Click **Save**

### 2. Email Forwarding

Forward receipts directly from your inbox:

1. Go to **Settings > Email Forwarding**
2. Copy your unique forwarding email address
3. Forward receipts to this address
4. Expenses are created automatically

### 3. Email Inbox Integration

Connect your email account for automatic import:

1. Go to **Settings > Email Accounts**
2. Connect your Gmail, Outlook, or other email provider
3. Smartbills scans for receipt emails automatically
4. Review and confirm imported expenses

### 4. Mobile App

Capture receipts on-the-go:

1. Open the Smartbills mobile app
2. Tap the camera icon
3. Take a photo of your receipt
4. Confirm or edit the extracted data
5. Save the expense

### 5. Manual Entry

For expenses without receipts:

1. Click **Add Expense**
2. Select **Manual Entry**
3. Fill in all required fields
4. Provide justification for missing receipt
5. Click **Save**

## Expense Categories

Common expense categories include:

- **Travel** - Flights, hotels, car rentals
- **Meals & Entertainment** - Client dinners, team lunches
- **Transportation** - Taxis, rideshares, parking
- **Office Supplies** - Stationery, equipment
- **Software & Subscriptions** - SaaS tools, licenses
- **Professional Services** - Consulting, legal fees
- **Telecommunications** - Phone, internet bills
- **Marketing** - Advertising, promotional materials

<Tip>
Configure custom categories in **Settings > Categories** to match your chart of accounts.
</Tip>

## Bulk Actions

Perform actions on multiple expenses at once:

1. Select expenses using checkboxes
2. Choose an action from the bulk menu:
   - **Categorize** - Assign category to all selected
   - **Add to Report** - Add all to an expense report
   - **Export** - Download as CSV or PDF
   - **Delete** - Remove selected expenses

## Best Practices

<Tip>
**Capture receipts immediately** - Upload receipts as soon as you receive them to avoid losing them.
</Tip>

<Tip>
**Use descriptive notes** - Add context to expenses to make approval faster.
</Tip>

<Tip>
**Review OCR results** - Always verify automatically extracted data for accuracy.
</Tip>

<Tip>
**Categorize consistently** - Use the same categories for similar expenses.
</Tip>
