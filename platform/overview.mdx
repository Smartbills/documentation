## What is Smartbills?

Smartbills is a comprehensive expense management platform that helps businesses and individuals track, manage, and report expenses efficiently. From receipt capture to expense report submission and approval workflows, Smartbills streamlines your entire expense management process.

## Platform Components

### Dashboard

The Smartbills dashboard provides a centralized view of all your expense management activities:

- **Home** - Overview of recent activity, pending tasks, and key metrics
- **Expenses** - View, upload, and manage individual expenses
- **Reports** - Create, submit, and track expense reports
- **Approvals** - Review and approve pending expense reports
- **Analytics** - Insights and spending trends

### Mobile App

Available for iOS and Android, the mobile app lets you:

- Scan receipts using your camera with OCR technology
- Create expenses on-the-go
- Submit expense reports from anywhere
- Receive push notifications for approvals and updates

### Email Inbox

Connect your email accounts to automatically:

- Import receipts from vendors
- Extract expense data using AI
- Organize expenses by category
- Reduce manual data entry

## User Roles

| Role | Capabilities |
|------|-------------|

| **Administrator** | Configure settings, manage users, set policies |

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.smartbills.io](https://app.smartbills.io) and create your business profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Add employees and configure their roles and departments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure categories">
    Set up expense categories that match your chart of accounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up approval workflows">
    Define who needs to approve expenses and under what conditions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start tracking expenses">
    Upload receipts, import from email, or use the mobile app.
  </Step>
</Steps>
