## What are Invoices?

Invoices in Smartbills are documents you send to customers to request payment for goods or services.

## Invoice vs Bill

| Invoice | Bill |
|---------|------|
| You send to customer | You receive from vendor |
| Accounts receivable | Accounts payable |
| Money coming in | Money going out |

## Creating Invoices

### New Invoice

1. Go to **Invoices**
2. Click **Create Invoice**
3. Select customer
4. Add line items
5. Set payment terms
6. Send or save as draft

## Invoice Fields

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Customer | Who you're billing |
| Invoice Number | Auto-generated or custom |
| Issue Date | Invoice date |
| Due Date | Payment deadline |
| Line Items | Products/services |
| Tax | Applicable taxes |
| Notes | Additional information |

## Invoice Status

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Draft | Not yet sent |
| Sent | Delivered to customer |
| Viewed | Customer opened |
| Paid | Payment received |
| Overdue | Past due date |
| Void | Cancelled |

## Features

- **Templates** - Custom invoice designs
- **Recurring** - Auto-generate recurring invoices
- **Online Payment** - Accept cards/ACH
- **Reminders** - Automatic payment reminders
- **Reporting** - AR aging reports

## Best Practices

<Tip>
**Send promptly** - Invoice as soon as work is complete.
</Tip>

<Tip>
**Clear terms** - Specify payment due date and methods.
</Tip>

<Tip>
**Follow up** - Send reminders for overdue invoices.
</Tip>
