## Overview

Budget notifications alert stakeholders when spending reaches predefined thresholds. This helps prevent surprise overruns and enables proactive budget management.

## Setting up notifications

From a budget's **Settings** page:

1. Click **Add Notification**.
2. Enter a **threshold percentage** (e.g., 75, 90, 100).
3. Save. The notification fires when actual spending crosses that percentage of the total budget.

### Common thresholds

| Threshold | Purpose |
|---|---|
| 50% | Early awareness of spending pace |
| 75% | Warning that budget is being consumed quickly |
| 90% | Urgent alert before budget exhaustion |
| 100% | Budget fully consumed |

## Notification delivery

Notifications are delivered through the Smartbills notification system. Depending on your configuration, alerts may appear as:
- In-app notifications
- Email alerts
- Push notifications (mobile)
