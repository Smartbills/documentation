## Reimbursement Process

After an expense report is fully approved, it enters the reimbursement process.

## Reimbursement Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Approval Complete">
    All required approvals are received.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finance Processing">
    Finance team processes the reimbursement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Payment Scheduled">
    Payment is scheduled per company policy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Payment Sent">
    Funds transferred to employee.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirmation">
    Employee receives confirmation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reimbursement Methods

| Method | Timing | Setup Required |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| Direct Deposit | 1-3 business days | Bank account on file |
| Payroll | Next pay cycle | Automatic |
| Check | 5-7 business days | Mailing address |
| Wire Transfer | 1-2 business days | International employees |

### Setting Up Payment Method

1. Go to **Profile > Payment Settings**
2. Select preferred method
3. Enter required information:
   - Bank account and routing number (direct deposit)
   - Address (check)
4. Verify with test deposit if required

## Reimbursement Status

Track your reimbursement progress:

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Pending Processing** | Approved, waiting for finance |
| **Processing** | Finance is preparing payment |
| **Scheduled** | Payment date assigned |
| **In Transit** | Payment sent |
| **Completed** | Payment received |
| **Failed** | Payment issue (action needed) |

### Viewing Status

1. Go to **Expense Reports**
2. Open your approved report
3. View **Reimbursement Status** section
4. See payment details and timeline

## Payment Schedule

### Standard Schedule

| Day Approved | Payment Day |
|--------------|-------------|
| Monday - Wednesday | Friday |
| Thursday - Friday | Next Tuesday |

### Payroll Integration

If integrated with payroll:

- Reimbursements added to regular pay
- Shown as separate line item
- Timing follows pay schedule

## Multi-Currency Reimbursement

### Exchange Rate Handling

For expenses in foreign currencies:

1. Amount recorded in original currency
2. Converted at transaction date rate
3. Reimbursed in your home currency
4. Rate source: Smartbills daily rates

### Example

```
Original Expense: €150.00 EUR
Exchange Rate (Jan 15): 1.08
Reimbursement: $162.00 USD
```

### Rate Discrepancies

If you believe the rate is incorrect:

1. Note the discrepancy in comments
2. Provide supporting documentation
3. Finance can adjust if needed

## Tax Considerations

### Taxable vs Non-Taxable

| Category | Tax Treatment |
|----------|---------------|
| Business travel | Non-taxable reimbursement |
| Meals (actual) | Non-taxable up to limits |
| Per diem | Non-taxable at federal rates |
| Commuting | May be taxable |
| Personal use | Taxable as income |

### Tax Documentation

Smartbills provides:

- Annual expense summary
- Tax category breakdown
- Exportable reports for tax filing

## Reimbursement Limits

### Per Category

| Category | Monthly Limit | Per Expense |
|----------|---------------|-------------|
| Meals | $500 | $75 |
| Travel | $5,000 | Varies |
| Equipment | $1,000 | $500 |

### Exceeding Limits

Expenses over limits:

1. Require additional approval
2. May be partially reimbursed
3. Excess may be employee responsibility

## Failed Payments

### Common Issues

| Issue | Resolution |
|-------|------------|
| Incorrect bank details | Update payment info, reprocess |
| Account closed | Provide new account |
| International issues | Wire transfer instead |
| Name mismatch | Verify account holder name |

### Resolving Failed Payments

1. You receive notification of failure
2. Go to **Profile > Payment Settings**
3. Correct the issue
4. Finance reprocesses payment
5. Monitor for completion

## Partial Reimbursement

When not all expenses are reimbursable:

### Non-Reimbursable Items

- Personal portions of split expenses
- Policy violations
- Missing required documentation
- Unapproved expenses

### Split Reports

Reimbursement may be partial:

```
Total Report: $500.00
Approved/Reimbursable: $450.00
Non-Reimbursable: $50.00

Reimbursement Amount: $450.00
```

## Reimbursement Reports

### For Employees

View your reimbursement history:

1. Go to **Reports > My Reimbursements**
2. Filter by date range
3. Export for personal records

### For Finance

Track all reimbursements:

1. Go to **Finance > Reimbursements**
2. View pending and completed
3. Export for accounting
