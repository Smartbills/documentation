## What are Bills?

Bills in Smartbills represent invoices from vendors that your business needs to pay. The bills feature provides a complete accounts payable workflow from receipt to payment.

## Bills vs Expenses

| Bills | Expenses |
|-------|----------|
| Vendor invoices | Employee purchases |
| Business pays vendor | Employee paid, needs reimbursement |
| Accounts payable | Expense reports |
| Net payment terms | Immediate |

## Bill Lifecycle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Receive">
    Upload or email-forward vendor invoice
  </Step>
  <Step title="Process">
    AI extracts invoice data
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    Verify and categorize
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve">
    Route through approval workflow
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Schedule and process payment
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Bill Status

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Draft** | Uploaded, needs review |
| **Pending Approval** | Awaiting approval |
| **Approved** | Ready for payment |
| **Scheduled** | Payment scheduled |
| **Paid** | Payment completed |
| **Overdue** | Past due date |

## Creating Bills

### Upload Invoice

1. Go to **Bills** in the sidebar
2. Click **Add Bill**
3. Upload the invoice PDF/image
4. AI extracts vendor, amount, due date
5. Review and confirm details

### Email Forward

1. Forward invoices to your bills email
2. Bills created automatically
3. Review in the inbox

### Manual Entry

1. Click **Add Bill > Manual Entry**
2. Enter vendor, amount, due date
3. Attach invoice document
4. Save

## Bill Fields

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Vendor | Who you owe |
| Invoice Number | Vendor's reference |
| Amount | Total due |
| Due Date | Payment deadline |
| Terms | Net 30, Net 60, etc. |
| Category | GL account |
| Description | Notes |

## Approval Workflow

Bills follow configurable approval workflows:

- Amount thresholds
- Department routing
- Multi-level approval
- Automatic for recurring vendors

## Payment Processing

### Payment Methods

- ACH transfer
- Check
- Credit card
- Wire transfer

### Scheduling

- Pay on due date
- Pay early (for discounts)
- Batch payments
- Recurring payments
