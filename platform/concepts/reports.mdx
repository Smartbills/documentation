## What is an Expense Report?

An expense report is a formal document that bundles multiple individual expenses together for submission, approval, and reimbursement. It serves as the primary mechanism for employees to request reimbursement for business expenses.

## Why Use Reports?

### Benefits

| Benefit | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Organization** | Group related expenses logically |
| **Efficiency** | Process multiple expenses at once |
| **Accountability** | Clear audit trail |
| **Approval** | Single workflow for multiple items |
| **Compliance** | Enforce policies consistently |

## Report Structure

### Header Information

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Report: Q1 2025 Business Travel         │
│ Submitted by: John Smith                │
│ Department: Engineering                 │
│ Date Range: Jan 1 - Mar 31, 2025        │
│ Total: $2,450.00                        │
└─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Line Items

Each expense in the report:

| Expense | Date | Amount | Category | Receipt |
|---------|------|--------|----------|---------|
| Flight to NYC | Jan 15 | $450 | Airfare | ✓ |
| Hotel - Hilton | Jan 15-17 | $600 | Lodging | ✓ |
| Client dinner | Jan 16 | $150 | Meals | ✓ |

## Report Types

### By Purpose

| Type | Use Case | Typical Content |
|------|----------|-----------------|
| **Travel** | Business trips | Flights, hotels, meals |
| **Monthly** | Regular expenses | Various categories |
| **Project** | Client work | Project-specific costs |
| **Event** | Conferences | Registration, travel |

### By Scope

- **Individual** - Single employee's expenses
- **Team** - Shared team expenses
- **Department** - Department-wide costs

## Report Policies

### Submission Deadlines

| Policy | Deadline |
|--------|----------|
| Travel expenses | 30 days after trip |
| Monthly expenses | 5th of following month |
| Quarterly close | End of fiscal quarter |

### Amount Thresholds

| Level | Approval Required |
|-------|-------------------|
| Under $100 | Auto-approve |
| $100 - $500 | Manager |
| $500 - $2,000 | Manager + Director |
| Over $2,000 | VP approval |

## Approval Workflow

Reports flow through configured approval chains:

### Simple Workflow

Employee → Manager → Approved

### Complex Workflow

Employee → Manager → Department Head → Finance → Approved

### Conditional Workflow

- Under threshold: Fast-track approval
- Over threshold: Multi-level review
- Policy exception: Additional scrutiny

## Report Analytics

### Available Metrics

### Dashboard Widgets

- Pending approvals count

## Best Practices

<Tip>
**Group logically** - Create reports around trips, projects, or time periods.
</Tip>

<Tip>
**Submit promptly** - Don't let expenses accumulate.
</Tip>

<Tip>
**Be thorough** - Include all related expenses in one report.
</Tip>

<Tip>
**Add context** - Describe the business purpose clearly.
</Tip>
