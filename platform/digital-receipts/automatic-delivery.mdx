## Automatic Receipt Delivery

Smartbills automatically delivers digital receipts to customers without any action required at checkout. No scanning QR codes, no entering email addresses, no app to open.

## How Automatic Delivery Works

### Payment Card Linking

Customers link their payment cards to their Smartbills account:

1. Customer downloads Smartbills app
2. Adds their credit/debit cards
3. Makes purchases as normal
4. Receipts appear automatically

### Transaction Matching

When a purchase is made:

```
Customer pays with linked card
         ↓
POS sends transaction to Smartbills
         ↓
Smartbills matches card to customer
         ↓
Receipt delivered to customer's app
         ↓
Push notification sent
```

## Fallback Options

When automatic matching isn't possible:

### QR Code Scan

Display QR code on receipt or terminal:

1. Customer scans with Smartbills app
2. Receipt linked to their account
3. Future purchases auto-matched

### Phone Number Lookup

1. Customer provides phone number
2. Receipt sent via SMS link
3. Links to Smartbills account

### Email Delivery

1. Customer provides email
2. Receipt sent as rich email
3. Option to save to Smartbills

## Implementation

### For POS Integration

Send transaction data to Smartbills:

```json
{
  "transactionId": "txn_123",
  "cardLastFour": "4242",
  "amount": 45.99,
  "currency": "USD",
  "merchantId": "merch_abc",
  "items": [...],
  "timestamp": "2025-01-15T10:30:00Z"
}
```

### Response

```json
{
  "receiptId": "rcpt_789",
  "delivered": true,
  "customerId": "cust_456",
  "deliveryMethod": "push"
}
```
