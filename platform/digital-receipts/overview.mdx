## What are Digital Receipts?

Smartbills digital receipts are intelligent, interactive receipts that are automatically delivered to customers after every purchase. Unlike paper receipts that get lost or faded, digital receipts live in your customers' Smartbills wallet forever.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Customer Makes Purchase">
    Customer pays at your POS with their linked payment method
  </Step>
  <Step title="Automatic Matching">
    Smartbills matches the transaction to the customer's account
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receipt Delivered">
    Digital receipt is instantly sent to their Smartbills app
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customer Engages">
    Customer can view, share, or interact with the receipt
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Smart Receipt Features

### Item-Level Details

Every receipt includes detailed information:

- Product name and description
- Individual item prices
- Applied discounts and promotions
- Tax breakdown by item
- SKU and product codes

### Interactive Elements

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Product Links** | Click to view product pages |
| **Review Prompts** | Rate items directly from receipt |
| **Reorder** | One-tap reorder of items |
| **Share** | Split bills with friends/family |
| **Warranty** | Register products for warranty |

### Rich Media

- Store logo and branding
- Product images
- QR codes for returns
- Digital signatures

## Getting Started

### For Merchants

1. **Connect POS** - Integrate your point of sale system
2. **Customize Receipts** - Add your branding and messages
3. **Enable Features** - Turn on reviews, loyalty, etc.
4. **Go Live** - Start sending digital receipts

### For Customers

1. **Download App** - Get Smartbills from App Store or Google Play
2. **Link Payment** - Add credit/debit cards
3. **Shop Anywhere** - Make purchases at Smartbills merchants
4. **Receive Receipts** - Get receipts automatically
