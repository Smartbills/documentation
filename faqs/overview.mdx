## Getting Started

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I create a Smartbills account?">
    1. Visit [app.smartbills.io/register](https://auth.smartbills.io/sign-up)
    2. Enter your email address and create a password
    3. Verify your email address
    4. Complete your profile setup
    
    You can also sign up with Google or Microsoft SSO.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
    Yes! Smartbills offers a 14-day free trial with full access to all features. No credit card required to start.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="What devices can I use Smartbills on?">
    Smartbills is available on:
    - **Web**: Any modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
    - **iOS**: iPhone and iPad (iOS 14+)
    - **Android**: Android devices (Android 8.0+)
    
    Your data syncs automatically across all devices.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="How do I invite my team?">
    1. Go to **Settings** → **Users & Teams**
    2. Click **Invite User**
    3. Enter their email address and assign a role
    4. Click **Send Invitation**
    
    They'll receive an email to create their account.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Expenses

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I upload an expense?">
    There are several ways to upload expenses:
    
    1. **Web**: Drag and drop receipts or click to browse
    2. **Mobile**: Use the camera to snap a photo
    3. **Email**: Forward receipt emails to your Smartbills inbox
    4. **Email Capture**: Connect your email for automatic capture
    
    Our AI automatically extracts merchant, amount, and date from receipts.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="What file types are supported for receipts?">
    We support:
    - **Images**: JPG, JPEG, PNG, HEIC, GIF
    - **Documents**: PDF
    - **Maximum size**: 10MB per file
    
    For best results, ensure images are clear and well-lit.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="How accurate is the AI receipt scanning?">
    Our AI achieves:
    - **Merchant name**: 99% accuracy
    - **Amount**: 99% accuracy
    - **Date**: 98% accuracy
    - **Tax**: 95% accuracy
    
    We recommend reviewing AI-extracted data before submitting expenses.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Can I split an expense across categories?">
    Yes! To split an expense:
    1. Open the expense detail
    2. Click **Split Expense**
    3. Enter amounts for each portion
    4. Assign categories to each split
    5. Save
    
    This creates separate expense records linked to the original.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="What happens if I upload the wrong receipt?">
    You can replace the receipt:
    1. Open the expense
    2. Click on the receipt image
    3. Select **Replace**
    4. Upload the correct receipt
    
    If the expense is already in a submitted report, you'll need to recall the report first.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Expense Reports

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I create an expense report?">
    1. Go to **Reports** and click **Create Report**
    2. Enter a name and optional description
    3. Add expenses to the report
    4. Review the report summary
    5. Click **Submit for Approval** when ready
    
    See our [detailed guide](/platform/reports/create) for more information.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Can I edit a report after submitting it?">
    Not directly. Once submitted, reports are locked. You can:
    1. **Recall** the report (if not yet reviewed)
    2. Make your changes
    3. Resubmit
    
    Note: Recalling restarts the approval process.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="How long do approvals take?">
    Approval time depends on your organization's workflow. Typically:
    - Simple workflows: 1-2 business days
    - Multi-level approval: 3-5 business days
    
    You'll receive notifications as your report progresses through approval.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="What if my report is rejected?">
    If rejected:
    1. Review the rejection reason in the report comments
    2. Make the necessary corrections
    3. Create a new report or update and resubmit
    
    Common rejection reasons include missing receipts, incorrect categories, or policy violations.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Approvals

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I approve expense reports?">
    As a manager:
    1. Go to **Reports** → **Pending Approval**
    2. Click on a report to review
    3. Examine each expense and receipt
    4. Click **Approve**, **Reject**, or **Request Changes**
    
    You can also approve directly from email or Slack notifications.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Can I delegate my approval authority?">
    Yes! To set up delegation:
    1. Go to **Settings** → **Delegation**
    2. Select a delegate
    3. Set the date range
    4. Choose which report types to delegate
    
    Your delegate will receive approval notifications during the specified period.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't log in to my account">
    Try these steps:
    1. Check you're using the correct email
    2. Use **Forgot Password** to reset
    3. Clear browser cache and cookies
    4. Try a different browser
    5. Check status.smartbills.io for outages
    
    Still stuck? Contact support@smartbills.io
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="My receipts aren't uploading">
    Common issues:
    - **File too large**: Max 10MB per file
    - **Wrong format**: Use JPG, PNG, or PDF
    - **Connection issue**: Check internet connection
    - **Browser issue**: Try a different browser
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="The AI extracted incorrect information">
    The AI may need correction for:
    - Unusual receipt formats
    - Poor image quality
    - Handwritten receipts
    
    Simply edit the expense to correct any errors. Your corrections help improve the AI.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
