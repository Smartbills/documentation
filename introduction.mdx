## What is Smartbills?

Smartbills is an expense management platform that helps businesses of all sizes take control of their spending. From receipt capture to reimbursement, Smartbills automates the entire expense lifecycle so your team can focus on what matters.

## Who is Smartbills For?

### For Employees
- **Snap & Submit**: Take a photo of your receipt and submit expenses in seconds
- **Track Status**: Monitor approval status and get notified when your report is approved
- **Easy Reimbursement**: Get paid back faster with streamlined processes

### For Managers
- **Quick Approvals**: Review and approve expense reports from any device
- **Policy Enforcement**: Automatically flag out-of-policy expenses before they reach finance
- **Team Insights**: See spending trends and budget utilization across your team

### For Finance Teams
- **Automated Accounting**: Sync expenses directly to your accounting software
- **Audit Trail**: Complete transaction history with receipts and supporting documentation
- **Compliance**: Ensure tax compliance with proper categorization and receipt storage

### For Developers
- **REST API**: Build custom integrations with our RESTful API
- **Webhooks**: Receive real-time notifications for expense events
- **SDKs**: Get started quickly with client libraries for popular languages

## Security & Compliance

Your data security is our top priority:

- **Bank-Level Encryption**: All data encrypted at rest and in transit with AES-256
- **SOC 2 Type II Certified**: Independently audited security controls
- **GDPR Compliant**: Full compliance with European data protection regulations
- **Regular Audits**: Continuous security testing and penetration testing
- **Role-Based Access**: Granular permissions and access controls
