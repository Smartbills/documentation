## What is SmartBills?

SmartBills is a comprehensive expense management platform that helps businesses of all sizes automate expense tracking, streamline approval workflows, and simplify reimbursement processes. Whether you're a small team or an enterprise organization, SmartBills provides the tools you need to manage expenses efficiently.

## Who is SmartBills For?

### For Employees
- **Snap & Submit**: Take a photo of your receipt and submit expenses in seconds
- **Track Status**: Monitor approval status and get instant notifications
- **Easy Reimbursement**: Get paid back faster with streamlined processes

### For Managers
- **Quick Approvals**: Review and approve expense reports from any device
- **Policy Enforcement**: Automatically flag out-of-policy expenses
- **Team Insights**: See spending trends and budget utilization

### For Finance Teams
- **Automated Accounting**: Sync expenses directly to your accounting software
- **Audit Trail**: Complete transaction history with supporting documentation
- **Compliance**: Ensure tax compliance with proper categorization and receipts

### For Developers
- **Powerful API**: Build custom integrations with our RESTful API
- **Webhooks**: Receive real-time notifications for expense events
- **Comprehensive Docs**: Detailed documentation with code examples
