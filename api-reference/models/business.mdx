## Business

The Business object represents an organization or company account on Smartbills. A business is the top-level entity that owns employees, expenses, vendors, and expense reports.

## Properties

<ResponseField name="id" type="integer">
  Unique identifier for the business
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="name" type="string">
  Display name of the business
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="legalName" type="string">
  Official legal name of the business entity
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="slug" type="string">
  URL-friendly identifier for the business (unique, lowercase, hyphenated)
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="currency" type="string">
  Default currency for the business (ISO 4217 code, e.g., `USD`, `CAD`, `EUR`)
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="timezone" type="string">
  Business timezone in IANA format (e.g., `America/New_York`, `Europe/London`)
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="address" type="object">
  Business mailing address
  <Expandable title="Address Object">
    <ResponseField name="street" type="string">
      Street address
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="city" type="string">
      City
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="state" type="string">
      State or province
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="postalCode" type="string">
      Postal or ZIP code
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="country" type="string">
      Two-letter ISO 3166 country code
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="logo" type="string">
  URL of the business logo image
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="plan" type="string">
  Current subscription plan. Possible values: `free`, `starter`, `professional`, `enterprise`
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="createdAt" type="string">
  Timestamp when the business was created (ISO 8601)
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="updatedAt" type="string">
  Timestamp when the business was last updated (ISO 8601)
</ResponseField>

## Example

```json
{
  "id": 456,
  "name": "Acme Corp",
  "legalName": "Acme Corporation Inc.",
  "slug": "acme-corp",
  "currency": "USD",
  "timezone": "America/New_York",
  "address": {
    "street": "100 Main Street, Suite 400",
    "city": "New York",
    "state": "NY",
    "postalCode": "10001",
    "country": "US"
  },
  "logo": "https://cdn.smartbills.io/businesses/acme-logo.png",
  "plan": "professional",
  "createdAt": "2023-01-15T10:00:00Z",
  "updatedAt": "2024-01-10T08:00:00Z"
}
```
