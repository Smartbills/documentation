No more asking for receipts or entering email addresses:

- Pay with your linked card
- Receipt arrives in seconds
- Push notification alerts you
- Full itemized details included

### Categories

Your receipts are automatically organized:

- 🍔 **Restaurants** - Dining and takeout
- 🛒 **Groceries** - Supermarkets and food
- ⛽ **Gas & Auto** - Fuel and car services
- 🛍️ **Shopping** - Retail purchases
- 💊 **Health** - Pharmacy and medical
- ✈️ **Travel** - Transportation and lodging

### Bill Splitting

Split any receipt with friends:

1. Open the receipt
2. Tap **Split Bill**
3. Select who owes what
4. Send payment requests
5. Track who has paid

### Search Everything

Find any receipt instantly:

- Search by merchant name
- Search by item purchased
- Search by date range
- Search by amount

### Export for Taxes

Need receipts for tax returns or expense reports?

1. Select receipts to export
2. Choose format (PDF, CSV)
3. Download or email

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Smartbills free?

Yes! The Smartbills app is completely free for consumers.

### How do I add my payment cards?

1. Open the app
2. Go to Settings > Payment Cards
3. Tap Add Card
4. Scan or enter card details

### What if a merchant isn't on Smartbills?

You can still scan and upload paper receipts manually. Just use the camera feature to capture any receipt.

### Can I delete receipts?

Yes, you can archive or delete any receipt. We recommend archiving for tax purposes.

### How do I contact support?

Tap Help in the app menu or email support@smartbills.io
