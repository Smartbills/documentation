## Link Your Payment Cards

### Why Link Cards?

Linking your cards enables automatic receipt delivery. When you pay with a linked card at a Smartbills merchant, your receipt appears automatically.

### How to Link a Card

1. Go to **Settings > Payment Cards**
2. Tap **Add Card**
3. Choose method:
   - **Scan** - Use camera to scan card
   - **Manual** - Enter card details
4. Verify if required

### Supported Cards

- Visa
- Mastercard
- American Express
- Discover
- Most debit cards

### Security

- Card numbers are encrypted
- We never store full card numbers
- Bank-level security
- PCI DSS compliant

## Troubleshooting

### Receipt didn't arrive?

1. Check if the merchant uses Smartbills
2. Verify your card is linked
3. Wait a few minutes (can take up to 1 hour)
4. Contact support if issue persists

### Can't link card?

1. Ensure card is active
2. Check card details are correct
3. Try scanning instead of manual entry
4. Contact your bank if issues continue
