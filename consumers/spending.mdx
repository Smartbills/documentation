## Categories

### Automatic Categorization

Purchases are automatically categorized:

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| 🍔 Restaurants | Dining out, takeout, delivery |
| 🛒 Groceries | Supermarkets, food stores |
| ⛽ Transportation | Gas, rideshare, parking |
| 🛍️ Shopping | Retail, online purchases |
| 🏠 Home | Hardware, furnishing |
| 💊 Health | Pharmacy, medical |
| 🎬 Entertainment | Movies, games, events |
| 📱 Subscriptions | Streaming, software |

### Custom Categories

Create your own categories:

1. Go to **Settings > Categories**
2. Tap **Add Category**
3. Name it and choose an icon
4. Assign receipts manually

## Budgets

### Set a Budget

1. Go to **Budgets**
2. Tap **Create Budget**
3. Choose category or overall
4. Set monthly limit
5. Enable alerts

### Budget Alerts

Get notified when:

- 50% of budget used
- 80% of budget used
- Budget exceeded

## Export Data

### For Taxes

Export receipts for tax deductions:

1. Go to **Export**
2. Select date range
3. Choose categories
4. Download as PDF or CSV

### For Expense Reports

Export for work reimbursement:

1. Select work-related receipts
2. Tap **Export for Expense Report**
3. Choose format
4. Email to yourself

## Privacy

Your spending data is:

- **Private** - Only you can see it
- **Encrypted** - Protected at all times
- **Not sold** - We never sell your data
- **Deletable** - You can delete anytime
