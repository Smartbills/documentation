## Overview

Creating a Smartbills account takes just a few minutes. Follow the steps below to sign up, verify your email, and complete your profile so you are ready to start managing expenses.

<Note>
  **Free to start** -- No credit card is required. You can begin with the free plan and upgrade at any time.
</Note>

## Account Creation Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the Sign-Up Page">
    Open your browser and navigate to [auth.smartbills.io/sign-up](https://auth.smartbills.io/sign-up). You can also click **Get Started** from the Smartbills homepage.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/guides/web-app/create-account/sign-up-page.png" alt="Smartbills sign-up page" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter Your Email and Password">
    Fill in the registration form:

    - **Email Address** -- Use your work email so your organization can easily find and invite you
    - **Password** -- Must be at least 8 characters; use a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols

    You can also sign up using **Google** or **Microsoft SSO** if your company has it enabled.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/guides/web-app/create-account/email-password.png" alt="Registration form with email and password fields" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify Your Email">
    After submitting the form, Smartbills sends a verification email to the address you provided.

    1. Open your email inbox
    2. Find the email from **noreply@smartbills.io**
    3. Click the **Verify Email** button in the message

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/guides/web-app/create-account/verify-email.png" alt="Verification email with Verify Email button" />
    </Frame>

    <Tip>
      If you do not see the email, check your spam or junk folder. You can also click **Resend** on the sign-up page.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete Your Profile">
    Once verified, you will be redirected to the profile setup screen. Fill in:

    - **First Name** and **Last Name**
    - **Phone Number** (optional but recommended for notifications)
    - **Profile Photo** (optional)

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/guides/web-app/create-account/complete-profile.png" alt="Profile completion form with name and phone fields" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose Your Plan">
    Select the plan that fits your needs:

    - **Free** -- For individuals or small teams getting started
    - **Pro** -- Advanced features including custom approval workflows
    - **Enterprise** -- Dedicated support, SSO, and advanced security

    You can change your plan at any time from **Settings > Billing**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/guides/web-app/create-account/choose-plan.png" alt="Plan selection screen showing Free, Pro, and Enterprise options" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  Your account is now active. You will be redirected to the Smartbills dashboard where you can set up your business or join an existing one.
</Check>

## Joining via Invitation

If a colleague has already set up a business on Smartbills, they can send you an email invitation:

1. Open the invitation email
2. Click **Accept Invitation**
3. Create your account or sign in with an existing one
4. You will be added to the business automatically

<Note>
  Invitation links expire after 7 days. Ask your administrator to resend the invite if it has expired.
</Note>
