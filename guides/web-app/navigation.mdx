## Overview

The Smartbills dashboard is designed to keep everything within reach. This guide explains each area of the interface so you can navigate confidently and find what you need quickly.

## Left Sidebar

The sidebar is divided into three groups: **Personal**, **Business**, and **System**. It remains visible on desktop and collapses into a menu on smaller screens.

### Personal Section

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Home" icon="house">
    Your personalized landing page. It displays:

    - A summary of recent activity
    - Pending tasks such as expenses to review or reports awaiting submission
    - Quick-action buttons for common tasks
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Wallet" icon="wallet">
    Your personal expense wallet. This is where you:

    - Upload receipts and create expenses
    - View, filter, and sort your expense list
    - Edit expense details and attach documents

    [Learn more about the Wallet](/guides/web-app/wallet/overview)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My Expense Reports" icon="file-invoice">
    Manage your own expense reports:

    - Create new reports and add expenses to them
    - Submit reports for approval
    - Track report status (Draft, Submitted, Approved, Rejected)
    - Recall submitted reports if changes are needed

    [Learn more about Expense Reports](/guides/web-app/reports/overview)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Business Section

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Expenses (Inbox)" icon="inbox">
    The company-wide expense inbox. Visible to managers, finance, and admin users. Use it to:

    - Review expenses submitted by team members
    - Filter by employee, date, status, or category
    - Export expense data for accounting

    [Learn more about Company Expenses](/guides/web-app/inbox/overview)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Bills" icon="file-invoice-dollar">
    Upload and manage vendor bills and invoices:

    - Upload bills with AI-powered data extraction
    - Route bills through approval workflows
    - Track payment status

    [Learn more about Bills](/guides/web-app/bills/overview)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Vault" icon="vault">
    A secure digital storage area for important documents:

    - Store receipts, contracts, and financial records
    - Search and filter by date, type, or keyword
    - Organize documents into folders

    [Learn more about the Vault](/guides/web-app/vault/overview)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Vendors" icon="store">
    Manage your vendor directory:

    - Add vendor profiles with contact and payment details
    - View spending history per vendor
    - Link vendors to expenses and bills automatically

    [Learn more about Vendors](/guides/web-app/vendors/overview)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Team" icon="users">
    Manage your organization's members:

    - Invite new team members by email
    - Assign roles (Employee, Manager, Admin)
    - View and update member profiles

    [Learn more about Team Management](/guides/web-app/team/overview)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Approvals" icon="check-double">
    Review pending expense reports and bills:

    - Approve, reject, or request changes on reports
    - View the full approval history and audit trail
    - Add comments for the submitter

    [Learn more about Approvals](/guides/web-app/approvals/overview)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### System Section

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Settings" icon="gear">
    Configure your business and account preferences:

    - **General** -- Business name, address, currency, and timezone
    - **Categories** -- Create and manage expense categories
    - **Departments** -- Define organizational departments
    - **Email Accounts** -- Configure receipt forwarding addresses
    - **Billing** -- Manage your subscription plan and payment method
    - **Branding** -- Customize logos and colors for reports

    [Learn more about Settings](/guides/web-app/settings/general)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Top Bar

The top bar is always visible and provides three key controls:

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Business Selector** | A dropdown in the top-left that lets you switch between businesses if you belong to more than one organization. The current business name and logo are displayed. |
| **Notifications** | A bell icon showing unread alerts. Click it to view approvals, comments, status changes, and system announcements. |
| **User Menu** | Your avatar in the top-right corner. Click to access your profile, preferences, language settings, and the sign-out option. |

## Main Content Area

The central panel changes based on your sidebar selection. Common patterns include:

- **List views** with search, filters, and sortable columns (Wallet, Expenses, Bills)
- **Detail panels** that slide in from the right when you click an item
- **Forms** for creating or editing records (expense reports, vendors, team members)
- **Dashboards** with charts and summary cards (Home)

## Dark and Light Mode

Smartbills supports both themes. To switch:

1. Open the **User Menu** in the top-right corner
2. Click **Theme** or use the toggle switch
3. Select **Light**, **Dark**, or **System** to match your OS preference

Your choice is saved to your profile and applies across all devices.

## Keyboard Shortcuts

Speed up your workflow with these shortcuts:

| Shortcut | Action |
|----------|--------|
| `Ctrl + K` / `Cmd + K` | Open the command palette to quickly navigate or search |
| `N` | Create a new expense (when in Wallet) |
| `R` | Create a new report (when in My Expense Reports) |
