## Overview

Uploading an expense is the most common action in the Wallet. Smartbills uses AI-powered OCR to automatically extract key details from your receipt -- including the merchant name, total amount, date, currency, and taxes -- so you spend less time on data entry.

<Note>
  **Supported file formats**: JPG, PNG, PDF, and HEIC. Maximum file size is 10 MB per receipt.
</Note>

## How to Upload an Expense

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Wallet">
    Click **Wallet** in the left sidebar. This opens your personal expense list.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/guides/web-app/wallet/upload-expense/navigate-wallet.png" alt="Sidebar with Wallet highlighted" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Add Expense">
    Click the **Add Expense** button in the top-right corner of the Wallet page. This opens the upload dialog.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/guides/web-app/wallet/upload-expense/add-expense-button.png" alt="Add Expense button in the wallet toolbar" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose Your Upload Method">
    You have three ways to add your receipt:

    - **Drag and drop** -- Drag a file from your computer directly into the upload area
    - **Browse files** -- Click the upload area to open your file browser and select a receipt
    - **Take a photo** -- On devices with a camera, use the camera option to capture a receipt in real time

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/guides/web-app/wallet/upload-expense/upload-methods.png" alt="Upload dialog showing drag-and-drop area, browse button, and camera option" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for OCR Processing">
    Once the file is uploaded, Smartbills processes it using AI-powered OCR. Within a few seconds, the system extracts:

    - **Merchant name** -- The vendor or store name
    - **Total amount** -- The final total including taxes
    - **Date** -- The transaction date
    - **Currency** -- Detected from the receipt
    - **Taxes** -- Individual tax amounts (e.g., GST, HST, PST, VAT)

    A processing indicator shows while the AI analyzes the receipt.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/guides/web-app/wallet/upload-expense/ocr-processing.png" alt="Receipt being processed with a loading indicator and extracted fields appearing" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review Extracted Data">
    After processing, review the extracted information. The AI is highly accurate, but you should verify the details:

    - Confirm the merchant name is correct
    - Check that the amount matches your receipt
    - Verify the date and currency
    - Review the tax breakdown

    Make corrections by clicking on any field and typing the correct value.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/guides/web-app/wallet/upload-expense/review-data.png" alt="Extracted expense data with editable fields alongside the receipt image" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Category, Department, and Notes">
    Enhance your expense with additional context:

    - **Category** -- Select a category from the dropdown (e.g., Travel, Meals, Office Supplies)
    - **Department** -- Assign the expense to a department if applicable
    - **Notes** -- Add any relevant context for your approver (e.g., client name, purpose of the meal)
    - **Payment Method** -- Indicate how you paid (corporate card, personal card, cash)

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/guides/web-app/wallet/upload-expense/add-details.png" alt="Category dropdown, department selector, and notes field" />
    </Frame>

    <Tip>
      Smartbills can automatically suggest a category based on the merchant. This feature improves over time as you categorize more expenses.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save the Expense">
    Click **Save** to add the expense to your Wallet. The expense will appear in your list with a **Draft** status, ready to be added to an expense report.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/guides/web-app/wallet/upload-expense/save-expense.png" alt="Save button and the new expense appearing in the wallet list" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  Your expense has been created and saved to your Wallet. You can now edit it, categorize it further, or add it to an expense report.
</Check>

## Uploading Multiple Receipts

You can upload several receipts at once by selecting multiple files in the file browser or dragging multiple files into the upload area. Each file creates a separate expense, and all of them are processed by OCR in parallel.
