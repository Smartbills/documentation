## What are Roles and Permissions?

Roles and permissions control what each user can see and do within your Smartbills organization. Every user is assigned a role, and each role grants a specific set of permissions. This ensures that employees can only access the features they need, managers can review and approve expenses, and administrators have full control over organization settings.

Roles are managed from **Settings > Team** (`/:slug/settings/team`).

## Available Roles

Smartbills provides five predefined roles, each designed for a specific function within the expense management workflow.

### Owner

The Owner is the person who created the Smartbills organization. There is exactly one Owner per organization. The Owner has unrestricted access to every feature and setting, including billing management and the ability to transfer ownership.

### Admin

Admins have full access to all features except billing and ownership transfer. They can manage team members, configure approval workflows, create categories and departments, and access all expenses and reports across the organization. Admins are typically finance leaders or operations managers.

### Manager

Managers can view and approve expenses for their direct reports. They have access to the Approvals pages and can see spending data for their team. Managers cannot change organization settings or manage other team members' roles.

### Employee

Employees are the most common role. They can create and manage their own expenses, build expense reports, and submit them for approval. Employees can only see their own data and cannot access other users' expenses or organization-wide reports.

### Accountant

Accountants have read access to all expenses, reports, and the Vault for audit and compliance purposes. They can review and categorize expenses in the Company Inbox but cannot change organization settings or manage team members.

## Permission Groups

Permissions in Smartbills are organized into groups. Each permission has a `VIEW` variant (read-only access) and a `MANAGE` variant (read and write access).

### Expense Permissions

| Permission | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| `EXPENSES_VIEW` | View expenses in the Personal Wallet |
| `EXPENSES_MANAGE` | Create, edit, and delete expenses in the Personal Wallet |
| `BUSINESS_EXPENSES_VIEW` | View expenses in the Company Expenses Inbox |
| `BUSINESS_EXPENSES_MANAGE` | Create, edit, categorize, and manage expenses in the Company Inbox |

### Bills Permissions

| Permission | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| `BILLS_VIEW` | View bills and invoices |
| `BILLS_MANAGE` | Create, edit, approve, and manage bills |

### Vendor Permissions

| Permission | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| `VENDORS_VIEW` | View the vendor directory and vendor details |
| `VENDORS_MANAGE` | Create, edit, merge, and delete vendor records |

### Vault Permissions

| Permission | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| `VAULT_VIEW` | View and search documents in the Vault |

### Settings Permissions

| Permission | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| `SETTINGS_TEAM_VIEW` | View the team roster and user roles |
| `SETTINGS_GENERAL_MANAGE` | Manage organization settings including categories, departments, approval workflows, and integrations |

## How to Assign Roles

Roles are assigned when inviting a new team member or by editing an existing member's profile in **Settings > Team**.

1. Navigate to **Settings > Team** (`/:slug/settings/team`)
2. To change a role: click on the team member's name and select a new role from the dropdown
3. To invite a new member: click **Invite**, enter their email address, and select their role

<Note>
  Only users with the `SETTINGS_GENERAL_MANAGE` permission (Owners and Admins) can invite team members and change roles.
</Note>

## Key Terminology

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Role** | A predefined set of permissions assigned to a user (Owner, Admin, Manager, Employee, Accountant) |
| **Permission** | A specific capability that grants access to a feature or action |
| **VIEW** | A permission level that allows reading data without making changes |
| **MANAGE** | A permission level that allows creating, editing, and deleting data |
| **Team** | All users in your Smartbills organization |
| **Invitation** | The process of adding a new user to your organization by email |

## Roles Involved

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| **Owner** | Full control. Manages billing, can transfer ownership, and has all Admin capabilities |
| **Admin** | Manages team members, configures settings, and has full access to all expense data |
| **Manager** | Approves expenses and reports for their team. Views team spending data |
| **Employee** | Manages their own expenses and submits reports for approval |
| **Accountant** | Read access to all expenses, reports, and the Vault for audit purposes |
