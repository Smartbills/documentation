## Overview

The Smartbills mobile app is designed to work even when you do not have an internet connection. You can capture receipts, create expenses, and edit existing expense data while offline. All changes are saved locally on your device and automatically synchronized with the Smartbills server when your connection is restored.

## What Works Offline

The following actions are fully available without an internet connection:

| Feature | Offline Behavior |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Scan receipts** | Capture receipts with your camera. Images are saved locally and OCR runs when you reconnect |
| **Create expenses** | Manually create new expenses with all fields (merchant, amount, date, category, notes) |
| **Edit expenses** | Modify any editable expense that was previously synced to your device |
| **View expenses** | Browse your expense list and view details for all previously loaded expenses |
| **Attach photos** | Add additional images to existing expenses |
| **View reports** | Access expense reports that were previously loaded |

<Tip>
  Open the Smartbills app while on Wi-Fi before traveling to ensure your most recent expenses and reports are cached on your device.
</Tip>

## What Requires a Connection

Some features require communication with the Smartbills server and are not available offline:

- **Submit expense reports** -- Reports cannot be submitted for approval while offline
- **View the company inbox** -- The shared Company Expenses Inbox requires a live connection to load
- **Approve or reject reports** -- Approval actions require server confirmation
- **Receive push notifications** -- Notifications are delivered over the internet
- **Search across all expenses** -- Offline search is limited to expenses cached on your device
- **Access team or admin settings** -- Settings changes require a server connection

<Note>
  If you attempt an action that requires a connection while offline, the app displays a message explaining that the action will be available once you reconnect.
</Note>

## How Offline Sync Works

### Saving Changes Locally

When you are offline, every action you take is saved to a local queue on your device. Each queued item includes a timestamp and the full details of the change. You can continue working normally -- the app does not block you from taking additional actions while previous changes are queued.

### Automatic Sync on Reconnect

When your device regains an internet connection, Smartbills automatically begins processing the offline queue. Changes are uploaded in the order they were created. You do not need to open the app or take any manual action to trigger the sync.

### Sync Indicator

The app displays a sync indicator to keep you informed of the current state:

| Indicator | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **Green checkmark** | All changes are synced. Your device is up to date |
| **Orange arrow (rotating)** | Sync is in progress. Changes are being uploaded |
| **Red exclamation mark** | One or more changes failed to sync. Tap for details |
| **Gray cloud with slash** | You are currently offline. Changes are queued locally |

The sync indicator appears at the top of the Home tab and on the Expenses tab when there are pending changes.

## Conflict Resolution

If the same expense is modified on both the mobile app (while offline) and the web dashboard (while the phone was disconnected), a conflict may occur. Smartbills uses a **last-write-wins** strategy to resolve conflicts:

- The most recent change -- based on the timestamp of when the edit was made -- takes precedence
- If you edited the merchant name on mobile at 2:15 PM and a colleague edited the category on the web at 2:20 PM, both changes are preserved because they affect different fields
- If you edited the amount on mobile at 2:15 PM and also edited the amount on the web at 2:20 PM, the web change (2:20 PM) overwrites the mobile change because it was more recent

<Warning>
  In rare cases where the same field is modified in both places, the older change is lost. Review your expenses after reconnecting if you suspect a conflict may have occurred.
</Warning>

## Managing Offline Data

### Storage

Offline data is stored securely on your device using encrypted local storage. Receipt images captured offline are saved at full resolution and uploaded when you reconnect. Be mindful of available device storage if you capture a large number of receipts while offline.

### Clearing the Offline Queue

Under normal circumstances, you should not need to clear the offline queue manually. If you encounter persistent sync errors:

1. Go to **Menu** > **Settings** > **Data & Storage**
2. Tap **View Sync Queue** to see all pending changes
3. You can retry failed items individually or tap **Retry All**

<Note>
  Do not uninstall the app while there are unsynced changes. Uninstalling removes all local data, including queued offline changes that have not yet been uploaded.
</Note>

## Best Practices

- **Sync before long trips**: Open the app on Wi-Fi to download the latest data before traveling to areas with limited connectivity
- **Check the sync indicator**: After reconnecting, confirm that all offline changes have been uploaded successfully
- **Avoid editing the same expense on multiple devices**: To prevent conflicts, make changes on one platform at a time
- **Keep the app updated**: The latest version of the app includes the most reliable offline sync engine
